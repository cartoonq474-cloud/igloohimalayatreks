Chitwan Jungle Safari Tour Overview

Our five-day Chitwan Jungle Safari Tour combines a two-night wildlife stay with travel from Kathmandu and your international arrival and departure. Chitwan National Park protects forests, grasslands and river habitats in Nepal’s southern Terai lowlands. The programme focuses on guided nature activities and Tharu cultural visits, with time beside the Rapti River during your stay.

After arriving in Kathmandu, you travel south by tourist bus to Chitwan. The road journey connects the capital’s hills with the warmer Terai plains. On arrival, your local activity briefing explains the programme and visitor rules. A walk around a Tharu village offers an introduction to local homes and daily life. The evening cultural programme features music and dance presented by members of the community for visiting guests.

Your full day in Chitwan includes a guided jungle walk, canoeing and birdwatching. Forest paths reveal different habitats, while the Rapti River provides another setting for observing birds and crocodiles. Rhinoceroses, deer and monkeys live in the park, alongside Bengal tigers. Every encounter depends on wildlife movements; neither sightings nor a particular species are guaranteed.

Before returning to Kathmandu, an Elephant Breeding Centre visit is planned around its opening hours and the onward transport schedule. The centre houses managed elephants, so this is a facility visit rather than a wild-elephant encounter. Your two Chitwan nights allow one full activity day between the road journeys. Weather and river conditions can require practical changes to the programme.

Accommodation includes two Kathmandu nights with breakfast and two Chitwan nights with meals during your stay. The five-day programme finishes with your Kathmandu departure after the return journey.

Read Before You Book

Before booking your Chitwan Jungle Safari Tour, consider the road journeys, outdoor conditions and planned activities. These details help you assess your comfort, prepare suitable belongings and discuss individual needs before travel.

Tour Difficulty and Fitness

The programme combines long road journeys with guided walks and river travel. Forest paths may be uneven, muddy or slippery, while heat and humidity can make short walks tiring. Boarding a canoe requires balance and may involve steps or an uneven riverbank, depending on river levels.

Regular walking helps you prepare for nature activities. Tell us about mobility limits, balance concerns or difficulties sitting for road journeys before booking.

Discuss children’s ages and accessibility needs before confirming the programme. The local operator must assess which activities are suitable. Wear comfortable footwear, carry drinking water and follow your guide’s advice about pace, rest stops and the chosen route.

Altitude and Lowland Conditions

This itinerary moves from Kathmandu to Chitwan’s lower and warmer plains. Its nature activities take place in a lowland setting, with no mountain walking stages or planned acclimatisation days.

Prepare for sun, humidity and insects during outdoor sessions. Carry drinking water, use insect repellent according to its label and wear suitable covering clothing. Tell your guide if you feel unwell, overheated or unusually tired, so the activity can stop and appropriate help can be arranged.

Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

If you have an existing medical condition, discuss the journey with a healthcare professional before travel. Ask about vaccinations, insect-bite precautions and any medicines you need to carry.

Arrange travel insurance that covers your wildlife activities, medical treatment and emergency transport in Nepal. Check the policy’s exclusions and assistance procedure with your insurer, including any restrictions on guided walks or canoeing. Carry your policy details and emergency contact number during travel.

Accommodation and Room Facilities

The package includes two nights in a three-star Kathmandu hotel with breakfast and two hotel nights in Chitwan. Your quotation identifies the chosen properties, room arrangement and requested upgrades before booking.

Chitwan hotel facilities vary, so ask about air conditioning, hot water, insect screens and bathroom arrangements before booking. Mention accessibility or room-sharing needs early. A national-park visit does not mean your hotel is inside the protected area.

Food and Dietary Requirements

Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included in Chitwan, with seasonal fruits when available. Kathmandu hotel nights include breakfast; other meals cost extra.

Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before booking so we can discuss arrangements with the hotel. Confirm arrival-day, departure-day and road-meal coverage, especially if travel times change the meal schedule.

Drinking Water

Carry a reusable bottle and drink safely bottled, boiled or appropriately treated water. River water or untreated tap water may still contain germs.

Boiling is the best way to kill germs in water. If boiling is unavailable, filtration followed by disinfection offers broader protection than many portable filters alone. Follow the instructions for treatment equipment or tablets.

Jungle Guide Support

A professional jungle guide accompanies nature activities and explains local habitats. Follow their instructions, remain with the group and keep away from wildlife. Carry your own daypack with drinking water and personal essentials.

Showers, Charging and Communication

Hotel facilities and connectivity vary between properties. Confirm hot water, Wi-Fi, charging access and any separately charged services with the selected hotel. Mobile reception may weaken during forest activities. Keep devices charged, protect them from water and ask permission before photographing local people during village visits.

Permits and Documents

Necessary entry permits and scheduled activity fees are included in the programme. Carry your passport or identification and keep entry documents for checks. Permission may be needed for professional filming or drones; confirm before bringing equipment.

Road Delays and Extra Time

Traffic, roadworks, landslides and heavy rain can delay the Kathmandu–Chitwan journeys. River levels and local restrictions can also change activity schedules during your stay.

Allow flexibility before international departure. We return to Kathmandu on Day 4 without a buffer day. Extra nights, transport or activities may cost more.

Weather and Best Time for the Chitwan Jungle Safari Tour

October to March generally offers more comfortable conditions for Chitwan’s outdoor activities. Hotter months require preparation for heat, while monsoon rainfall can affect forest access, canoeing and road travel. Wildlife remains unpredictable in every season, and activities depend on the conditions assessed during travel.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Valley to lowland transition

Kathmandu and Chitwan can feel different on the same trip. The capital lies in a higher valley, while Chitwan’s lowlands are generally warmer and more humid, especially during hotter months.

Winter mornings beside the river can feel cool or misty, even when afternoons become warmer. Pack a layer for early outings and sun protection for later activities. Wet weather can change river conditions and the suitability of forest paths.

Pack lightweight neutral clothing for jungle safaris and a warm fleece for early morning river safaris.

Which Season Should You Choose?

Wildlife activity & temperature priorities

Choose October to March if comfortable outdoor temperatures are your priority. Plan carefully for the heat from April to June. During the monsoon, keep expectations flexible and check which activities are operating before confirming your onward travel arrangements and dates.

Early morning and late afternoon safaris avoid midday subtropical heat in all seasons.

Chitwan Jungle Safari Tour FAQs

Everything you need to know about preparing for, booking, and enjoying Chitwan National Park activities.

General & Experience

Can I join without previous safari experience?

Yes, previous safari experience is unnecessary. You should be comfortable with the planned walks, road journeys and canoe boarding. Discuss mobility needs, children’s ages and health concerns before booking, then follow the jungle guide’s instructions during every activity.

Does this five-day tour include five days in Chitwan?

No. The package lasts five days including arrival and departure in Kathmandu. You spend two nights in Chitwan, with one full activity day between outward and return road journeys, plus shorter visits around the travel schedule.

Are rhinoceros or tiger sightings guaranteed?

No. Wild animals move freely, and sightings depend on their movements, habitat, weather and viewing conditions. Chitwan supports rhinoceroses and Bengal tigers, but the programme promises guided activities rather than an encounter with a particular species.

Where do we stay during the tour?

You spend two nights in a three-star Kathmandu hotel and two nights at the selected Chitwan accommodation. Your quotation confirms the properties and room arrangements. Staying near Chitwan National Park does not mean accommodation inside the protected area itself.

Is a jeep safari included in this itinerary?

A jeep safari is not part of this itinerary. The scheduled activities are jungle walks, canoeing, birdwatching and cultural visits. Ask about availability, timing and any extra cost before adding one to your confirmed programme.

Do I need a guide for the nature activities?

A professional jungle guide is included for the scheduled nature activities. Stay with the group and follow instructions on wildlife distance, walking routes and river outings. Do not enter forest trails independently during the programme.

What should I wear for walks and canoeing?

Wear comfortable closed shoes and lightweight, covering clothing suitable for the season. Bring drinking water, sun protection and insect repellent. Carry rain protection when needed. For canoeing, use a life jacket and follow the boat crew’s instructions.

Can I enquire as a solo traveller?

Yes. Send your preferred dates and group requirements so we can check available room and activity arrangements. Your quotation confirms the price for one traveller, the selected hotel and any room supplement before you book.

Which meals are included in the package?

The Chitwan stay includes breakfast, lunch and dinner, plus seasonal fruits when available. Kathmandu hotel nights include breakfast. Kathmandu lunches and dinners remain separate. Confirm road meals and the first and last Chitwan meals against your transfer schedule.

Can children or travellers with mobility needs join?

Suitability depends on the individual and the activities selected. Tell us children’s ages and any mobility, balance or health needs beforehand. Forest paths and canoe boarding may present difficulties; the local operator must assess arrangements and available alternatives.

Will all activities operate in every season?

Activities depend on weather, river levels, park access and local operating decisions. Heavy rain can interrupt canoeing and forest visits. The local team confirms arrangements; any replacement activity and related price change should be agreed with you.

How is the Elephant Breeding Centre visit arranged?

The visit is planned for Day 4 before the journey to Kathmandu. Its opening hours must fit your transport departure. Check the final schedule before booking; this is a visit to a managed facility and does not include elephant riding.
