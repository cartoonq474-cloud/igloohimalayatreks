01 • OVERVIEW

Honeymoon Tour Overview

Our eleven-day Honeymoon Tour begins and ends in Kathmandu, connecting Nepal’s cultural places with hill viewpoints, Pokhara and Chitwan. You spend a night in Nagarkot, visit Sarangkot at sunrise and stay in the Gurung village of Ghandruk. Private road transfers connect the destinations, with guided sightseeing and time to enjoy the journey as a couple.

The opening days explore Kathmandu Valley’s temples, stupas and old town squares before a stay in Nagarkot. Clear weather may reveal Himalayan peaks at sunrise. Returning to Kathmandu adds more heritage visits before the road journey west to Pokhara. Here, an early Sarangkot outing and sightseeing introduce the lake city and its surrounding hills, while time beside Phewa Lake lets you choose a slower pace between scheduled activities.

Ghandruk adds a village overnight to the holiday. Travel from Pokhara by road and explore on foot, allowing for steps and uneven paths between houses. Your guesthouse provides a simpler setting than a city hotel. Mountain views depend on the weather, while the visit introduces a different community and daily routine before returning to Pokhara.

The final part of the route heads to Chitwan for two nights and planned jungle activities. A canoe outing, birdwatching and Tharu cultural experiences introduce the lowland setting. The safari arrangements depend on the agreed programme, park access and local conditions. After returning to Kathmandu by road, spend your last night in Kathmandu before the airport transfer on Day 11.

This is a road-based couples’ tour. Confirm the room arrangements, all ten overnight stays and meal coverage before booking, including whether seasonal fruits or dinner refreshments are provided.

Walking and Road Travel

This is a touring itinerary with road transfers and sightseeing walks. You should be comfortable with steps, uneven paving, village paths and getting in and out of vehicles. Long drives can also be tiring. Share mobility needs before booking so we can discuss the programme together.

There is no multi-day trek in our holiday programme. Ghandruk is reached by road, followed by local walking and the village overnight stay.

Before travel, become comfortable walking for several hours with breaks, including steps and gentle hills. Wear shoes you already know. Ask about vehicle access, walking distances and property steps if mobility, balance or fatigue could affect your enjoyment.

Elevation and Sunrise Visits

Nagarkot, at approximately 2,175 metres, is the highest named overnight destination. Ghandruk is around 1,990 metres, while Sarangkot is a daytime viewpoint visited from your Pokhara accommodation before breakfast.

Hilltop visits can feel cold, particularly in winter. Bring warm clothing even if the previous afternoon was sunny. Walk at a comfortable pace, take breaks and tell your guide if you feel unwell. Discuss medical concerns beforehand rather than assuming a touring holiday requires no preparation.

Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

If you have a medical condition, discuss this route with a healthcare professional before travel. Mention road journeys, walking, the village stay and Chitwan activities when seeking advice.

Arrange insurance covering your planned sightseeing, village walking, road travel, medical care and emergency transport in Nepal. Check restrictions affecting canoeing or safari activities and keep the insurer’s assistance number accessible. Insurance, medical bills and policy excesses are outside the published package inclusions.

Accommodation and Room Arrangements

The route needs ten overnight stays: three in Kathmandu, one in Nagarkot, three in Pokhara, one in Ghandruk and two in Chitwan. Confirm every hotel category and room arrangement before booking.

Confirm that your quotation covers all three Kathmandu hotel nights, including the final stay before departure. Ghandruk uses a guesthouse; ask about bathrooms, heating, hot water and the walk from the vehicle to your room before booking.

Food and Dietary Requirements

Breakfast accompanies the hotel stays; Ghandruk and Chitwan provide full-board meals. Confirm arrival-day meal coverage and discuss dietary requirements before you travel.

Lunch and dinner in Kathmandu, Pokhara and Nagarkot are excluded. Seasonal fruits can be purchased; wash or peel them safely. Confirm included dinner refreshments and tell us about allergies before booking.

Drinking Water

Carry reusable bottles and use safely bottled, boiled or appropriately treated drinking water. Tap or river water is not necessarily safe to drink.

Ask your hotel or guide about refill arrangements and whether drinking water is included in your quotation. Keep water accessible during transfers and sightseeing, particularly in Chitwan’s warmer weather. Avoid ice of uncertain origin.

Guide and Local Activity Support

The package lists a professional English-speaking tour guide and guided sightseeing in Kathmandu and Pokhara. Confirm the guide’s accompaniment on road journeys and the local team responsible for Chitwan activities before travelling together.

Communication and Personal Belongings

Hotel Wi-Fi, charging access and mobile reception depend on the property and location. Keep your phone charged for transfer coordination, carry essential contacts offline and protect valuables during visits. Ask before photographing people or religious interiors, and follow local guidance about shoes, clothing and restricted access.

Admissions and Documents

Chitwan jungle activity entry fees are included; Kathmandu and Pokhara heritage admissions are excluded. Confirm whether any conservation permit is needed for Ghandruk and whether its cost is covered. Carry your passport and requested travel documents.

Road Conditions and Extra Time

Traffic, roadworks, heavy rain and local access can delay transfers. Keep travel flexible and avoid booking activities immediately after a long drive between destinations.

Your international departure follows a Kathmandu stay. For more protection against road disruption, discuss an extra night before flights and agree its separate cost.

REGIONAL CLIMATE VARIATIONS

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Chitwan can feel warmer than the hill destinations, especially in spring and summer. Nagarkot sunrise, Sarangkot’s viewpoint and Ghandruk evenings need warmth even when daytime sightseeing in Pokhara feels comfortable.

Rain can affect the road itinerary and make village paths slippery. Cloud may hide mountains on either sunrise outing. Carry rain protection, allow flexibility on driving days and check the Chitwan programme, particularly canoe and safari access, before activities.

Pack layers for cool 2,175 m ridge stops and warm 150 m jungle lowlands
COUPLES PLANNING ADVICE

Which Season Should You Choose

Choose autumn for opportunities for clearer hill views and spring for warmer days. Winter can suit Chitwan visits but requires warm clothing at sunrise. Monsoon travel needs greater flexibility for rain, road delays and changes to activities across the itinerary.
