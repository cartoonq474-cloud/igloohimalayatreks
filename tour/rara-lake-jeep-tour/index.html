Rara Lake Jeep Tour Overview

Our Rara Lake Jeep Tour follows a seven-day overland itinerary from Kathmandu into Karnali, with a full exploration day at the lake. You travel via Kohalpur and Manma, then drive towards Talcha before completing the final approach on foot. Rara lies at approximately 2,990 m / 9,810 ft inside Rara National Park in western Nepal.

Road travel occupies most of this seven-day holiday. Start with a long drive to Kohalpur, then continue towards Manma in Kalikot. Beyond the lower roads, expect bends, uneven surfaces and slower progress. The parking point depends on access and conditions, so confirm the walking approach and its timing before departure. The jeep connects your overnight stops, with the last section to Rara completed on foot from the roadhead.

At Rara, the programme leaves Day 4 for lakeshore walks, photography and time outdoors. Forest surrounds parts of the lake, and birds may be seen near the water. Your walking distance can reflect the weather and your energy. An excursion to Murma Top is optional and needs agreement; it is not required by the itinerary.

The return retraces the approach, with a walk back to the jeep followed by overnight stops in Manma and Kohalpur. Although the journey is by vehicle, you should be comfortable with consecutive driving days and uneven paths. Lake accommodation is simpler than city hotels. Confirm room arrangements, the meal plan, guiding support and permit coverage in your quotation before booking.

The package begins with the Kathmandu departure and ends after the return drive. Arrange any Kathmandu nights and airport transfers separately unless they appear in your confirmed quotation.

Rara Lake Jeep Tour Itinerary — Day by Day

This seven-day itinerary starts and finishes in Kathmandu, with six planned overnight stays along the route. Driving figures are approximate planning estimates; road conditions, stops and the final parking point can change daily timings.

Day 1: Kathmandu to Kohalpur by Jeep

Overland Highway Drive — Kohalpur (~175 m / 574 ft) — ~12 Hours Drive (~535 km)

MAIN ROUTE Kathmandu → Kohalpur
DRIVING TIME Approximately 12 hours; allow for stops and delays
ROAD DISTANCE Approximately 535 km; routing dependent
OVERNIGHT Kohalpur, approximately 175 m / 574 ft
ACCOMMODATION AND MEALS Property and cost coverage as confirmed in your quotation

Leave Kathmandu early for the long road journey to Kohalpur. Keep drinking water, snacks and essential medicines accessible, with suitable breaks during the day for meals, toilets and rest stops.

The drive forms the first stage of the westward approach rather than a sightseeing circuit. Traffic and roadworks can extend the timing, so allow the full day for travel. On arrival, settle into your agreed accommodation in Kohalpur. Check the next morning’s departure time and prepare your day bag before resting for another driving day.

Day 2: Kohalpur to Manma by Jeep

Karnali Highway Scenic Drive — Manma, Kalikot (~2,033 m / 6,670 ft) — ~7–8 Hours Drive (~250 km)

MAIN ROUTE Kohalpur → Surkhet → Manma
DRIVING TIME Approximately 7–8 hours; conditions dependent
ROAD DISTANCE Approximately 250 km
OVERNIGHT Manma, Kalikot
ACCOMMODATION AND MEALS As specified in your quotation

Continue towards Surkhet and join the Karnali Highway for Manma in Kalikot. The road becomes more winding as the journey moves from lower country towards the mountain districts.

Expect a mixture of paved sections and rougher patches, with progress depending on traffic, maintenance and the weather. Keep your seat belt fastened and take care when leaving the vehicle at roadside stops. Manma is the planned overnight stop before the final approach towards Rara. Prepare warmer clothing for the lake stay and keep essentials accessible.

Day 3: Drive towards Talcha and Walk to Rara Lake

Drive to Roadhead & Hike into National Park — Rara Lake (~2,990 m / 9,810 ft) — ~2 Hours Hike

OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Rara Lake area, approximately 2,990 m / 9,810 ft
ACCOMMODATION AND MEALS Lake property and coverage as confirmed in your quotation

Follow the road towards the Talcha area, with slower travel on rough sections. At the stopping point, leave the jeep and begin the walk to Rara, carrying the items needed for your lake stay.

The walking estimate is about two hours, although the roadhead and pace can change this. Reach the lakeshore accommodation arranged for your booking and take time to rest. Rara is higher than the earlier stops, so keep today’s activities gentle after arrival.

Day 4: Explore Rara Lake

Lakeshore Exploration, Nature Walks & Photography — Rara Lake (~2,990 m / 9,810 ft) — Full Day Exploration

MAIN ACTIVITY Lakeshore exploration and walks suited to conditions
WALKING TIME AND DISTANCE Flexible; agree the day’s walk locally
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Rara Lake area, approximately 2,990 m / 9,810 ft
OPTIONAL ACTIVITIES Murma Top, boating and horse riding require separate agreement
ACCOMMODATION AND MEALS Second planned Rara night; coverage as quoted

Spend the day exploring Rara at a comfortable pace. Walk along lakeshore paths, look for birds and choose places for photographs, allowing time to enjoy the water, forest and surrounding hills.

There is no summit hike or full circuit of the lake today. Choose a shorter walk if you prefer, and return to your accommodation for the second night. Boating, horse riding and a Murma Top excursion depend on local conditions and separate arrangements; none is automatically included in the programme.

Walk back from your lake accommodation to the agreed jeep meeting point. Check the departure time beforehand and keep essentials accessible, as the return road journey to Manma occupies the rest of the travel day.

Retrace the mountain approach, allowing for uneven surfaces and changes to road access. Stops depend on the day’s progress and suitable places along the route. Manma is again the planned overnight base. Settle into your accommodation and prepare for the drive towards Kohalpur tomorrow.

Day 6: Manma to Kohalpur by Jeep

ACCOMMODATION AND MEALS Final planned journey night; coverage as quoted

Continue back along the Karnali Highway towards Surkhet and Kohalpur. This is another full road travel day, with breaks arranged around route conditions rather than a fixed list of sightseeing visits or excursions en route.

The road returns towards lower elevations, where temperatures can feel warmer than at Rara. Keep a change of clothing and water nearby. Spend the final planned journey night in Kohalpur, ready for the long return drive to Kathmandu tomorrow morning.

The seven-day programme ends after the agreed Kathmandu drop-off. Avoid a tight onward connection that evening, as delays remain possible. A Kathmandu hotel night, airport transfer or later international departure is a separate arrangement unless your quotation includes it.

Consider consecutive driving days, road access, lake accommodation and the walking approach before booking. These points clarify the trip demands so you can prepare comfortably and understand what to expect beyond Nepal’s main highways.

Driving Demands and Walking Ability

Most of the journey is by jeep, but hours sitting and bends can be tiring. Rough sections may be uncomfortable for travellers with back or joint problems. The final approach involves walking on uneven paths, so the tour requires more mobility than a door-to-door city transfer.

Climbing skills are unnecessary. Comfortable footwear, steady balance and the ability to walk for around two hours help you manage the lake approach.

Tell us about mobility needs, children or difficulty with drives before booking. Vehicle access does not mean the jeep reaches your room. Confirm the roadhead, walking route and any luggage assistance rather than assuming an accessible lakeshore transfer.

Altitude and Health

Rara Lake sits at 2,990 m / 9,810 ft. Travelling by road brings a gain in altitude, and arriving by jeep does not remove the possibility of altitude illness.

Headache, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue after gaining height need attention. Tell your companion or guide and seek medical advice. Do not go higher while unwell; worsening symptoms require descent and medical help. Discuss health conditions and your planned altitude with a healthcare professional before travelling.

Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

Discuss health conditions with a healthcare professional before travel. Explain the long drives, remote location, lake elevation and walking, including any optional excursion higher than the lakeshore itinerary.

Arrange insurance covering road travel, walking and maximum altitude, with medical treatment and emergency transport in Nepal. Check assistance arrangements, exclusions and excesses. Local facilities and transport may be limited; do not assume rescue services or medical costs are included in your package.

Accommodation and Overnight Stops

Six overnight stops are planned: two each in Kohalpur, Manma and Rara. Confirm the properties, room sharing and booking coverage. Facilities beside the lake may be simpler than city hotel facilities.

Ask about bedding, toilets, washing water and electricity before booking. Do not assume an attached bathroom, continuous hot water or specified room facilities. Kathmandu nights before departure and after the drive require separate confirmation in your quotation.

Food and Dietary Requirements

Confirm the meal allowance and food before booking. Share dietary requirements early, particularly allergies, as choices may be limited near the lake.

Seasonal fruits can be bought as snacks where available. Wash them with safe water or peel them yourself. Complimentary fruit servings are included only if your quotation confirms that food allowance.

Drinking Water

Carry safe water during drives and walks. Use sealed bottled water or appropriately treated water; avoid drinking untreated lake, stream or tap water.

Keep water accessible, check refill options before leaving accommodation and carry enough for the walk. Confirm whether drinking water is covered by your quotation or paid separately, and take empty containers to disposal points.

Guide, Driver and Luggage Support

Agree who accompanies the tour, the guide’s language and duties, and driver arrangements before booking. Confirm how bags reach Rara after you leave the jeep; porter assistance should not be assumed unless arranged.

National Park Visits and Local Customs

Rara is a protected national park. Follow local visitor instructions, keep to permitted paths and carry rubbish to a collection point. Avoid disturbing wildlife or playing loud music. Ask permission before photographing people, and treat lakeshore settlements and religious places with care during your visit there.

Permits and Personal Spending

A Rara National Park entry permit is required. Confirm the current charge, how it is obtained and whether your quotation covers it. Carry identification and enough Nepali rupees for agreed local payments, drinks and personal purchases.

Road Access and Changes to the Programme

Rain, landslides, snow, roadworks and vehicle issues can interrupt access. Check the route before departure and leave room in your plans for travel delays.

Changed roadheads, extra nights or activities need discussion, including costs. Driving times are estimates, so avoid tight onward commitments after the planned Kathmandu return.

Weather and Best Time for the Rara Lake Jeep Tour

Spring and autumn are commonly chosen for Rara visits, with departure depending on road access and weather. The lake is cooler than the lower road stops. Rain can affect roads, while winter cold and snowfall may restrict access or make walking harder around Rara.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Elevation shifts & roadhead conditions

Kohalpur can feel warmer than Rara, particularly during sunny daytime stops. Higher roads and lake evenings need warmer clothing, with waterproof protection kept accessible rather than buried in the luggage.

Rain may slow travel on rough roads and make the walking approach slippery. Snow or ice can affect departures. Wind beside the lake can feel cold, so choose walks to suit conditions and keep time to return before dark.

Pack warm layers, a waterproof shell, and sturdy walking footwear accessible in your daypack during long road journeys.

Which Season Should You Choose

Overland access & seasonal timing

Consider spring or autumn, checking road access for dates before booking. Monsoon travel needs flexibility because rain can interrupt mountain roads. Winter requires warm equipment and confirmation of road and accommodation availability; a jeep cannot guarantee access in poor weather.

Rara Lake Jeep Tour FAQs

Essential answers about driving hours, walking approach, permits, accommodation and season choice.

Route & Walking Approach

How many days does the tour take?

The programme takes seven days, beginning with the Kathmandu to Kohalpur drive and ending with the return drive to Kathmandu. Six overnight stops are planned along the route. Airport arrival, international departure and Kathmandu nights are separate arrangements.

Does the jeep drive all the way to the lake?

No. The itinerary includes a final walk from the parking point to Rara. The approximate walking time is about two hours, but access, the roadhead and your pace can alter this. Confirm the approach before departure.

How much walking is involved?

Expect the approach walk to Rara, lakeshore exploration and the return walk to the jeep. Day 4 can follow a walking plan rather than a compulsory full lake circuit. Discuss mobility needs before booking your journey.

How high is Rara Lake?

Rara Lake is 2,990 m / 9,810 ft above sea level. This is the lake reference, not a confirmed maximum for every road alignment or optional hike. Ask about the route high points and any additional excursion before booking.

Is Murma Top included in the itinerary?

No mandatory Murma Top hike appears in this seven-day programme. Day 4 is for lake exploration. If you want the higher viewpoint, discuss walking time, conditions, altitude, support and additional costs before agreeing the excursion.

What accommodation should we expect?

The plan has two nights each in Kohalpur, Manma and Rara. Properties and room standards need confirmation. Lake facilities may be basic, so ask about toilets, bedding, washing water and electricity before confirming the stay.

Are meals and seasonal fruits included?

Meal coverage is not published as a detailed inclusion list. Your written quotation should identify any breakfasts, lunches, dinners, water or snacks covered. Bring or buy seasonal fruits where available; complimentary servings require confirmation before your booking.

What is the tour price?

Package prices and group rates are not published. Send your travel dates and party size for a quotation, then check the agreed transport, accommodation, meals, permits and support before paying for your seven-day Rara journey.

Do we need a national park permit?

Yes. Rara National Park requires an entry permit. Ask how it will be arranged, which charge applies to you and whether the cost is covered by your quotation. Keep the permit and identification available for checks during visits.

Are boating and horse riding included?

Neither activity is automatically included in this itinerary. Availability depends on local arrangements and conditions. Agree the operator, safety arrangements and price before taking part. Day 4 allows time for lakeshore walks, photographs and relaxation at your pace.

Can the tour run during monsoon or winter?

Ask about road access and accommodation before booking either season. Rain can disrupt mountain roads, while winter snow and ice can restrict travel. Keep plans flexible, prepare appropriate clothing and discuss what happens if access changes beforehand.

Can we fly internationally after returning on Day 7?

Avoid a tight connection after the return drive. Kohalpur to Kathmandu is a long journey, and traffic or road delays may extend it. Allow time afterwards and arrange a Kathmandu hotel or airport transfer separately unless your quotation includes them.
