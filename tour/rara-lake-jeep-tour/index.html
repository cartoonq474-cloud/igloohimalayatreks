Frequently Asked Questions for 7-Day Rara Lake Overland Jeep Tour

General Information

Why is Rara Lake considered Nepal's most pristine lake? ∨
Rara Lake is Nepal's largest and deepest freshwater lake (2,990m), surrounded by dense juniper and pine forests and snow-capped peaks. Located in remote Western Nepal, it remains untouched by commercial mass tourism.
What is the total duration and route of the overland jeep tour? ∨
The tour takes 7 days starting from Kathmandu or Nepalgunj, traveling via Surkhet, the scenic Karnali Highway, Kalikot, and Sinja Valley to Rara National Park.
Can this tour be customized with domestic flights? ∨
Yes! Travelers who prefer to avoid long overland driving can fly from Kathmandu to Nepalgunj and onwards to Talcha Airport (Mugu), followed by a gentle 2-hour walk to Rara Lake.
Are Rara National Park entry permits included? ∨
Yes, all national park permits and vehicle checkpost registrations are 100% arranged and included in your tour package.
What type of 4WD vehicle is used for the journey? ∨
We provide rugged, heavy-duty 4WD Mahindra Scorpio or Toyota Land Cruiser vehicles operated by experienced mountain drivers specialized in off-road western Himalayan roads.
Is airport transfer included in this tour package? ∨
Yes! We provide private AC car/van airport pickup and drop-off service for all flights.
What standard of accommodation is available around Rara Lake? ∨
Because Western Nepal is remote, accommodation consists of rustic, clean teahouses and eco-tented lakeside lodges in Rara, with basic attached or shared facilities and warm hospitality.
What meals are served during the overland tour? ∨
Wholesome, freshly prepared local organic meals: traditional Dal Bhat, organic Jumla red rice, buckwheat rotis, seasonal mountain vegetables, noodles, and hot herbal teas.
Is altitude sickness a concern at Rara Lake (2,990m)? ∨
At 2,990 meters, mild altitude effects can be noticed by some travelers. Because our overland driving route ascends gradually over several days, your body acclimatizes naturally. Drink plenty of water and stay well-rested.
What should I pack for Western Nepal's remote climate? ∨
Nights can be cold (especially October to April). Pack a warm down jacket, thermal base layers, a power bank (electricity can be intermittent in remote villages), and personal medications.
What is included in the overland package price? ∨
The tour includes private 4WD vehicle with fuel and experienced driver, national park permits, all meals and teahouse accommodation, and an English-speaking guide.
How much deposit is required to confirm? ∨
A 20% advance deposit secures your private 4WD jeep and western region logistics.
What historic significance does the Sinja Valley hold? ∨
En route to Rara, you pass through Sinja Valley, the historic capital of the medieval Khas Malla Empire (12th-14th century) and the ancient archaeological birthplace of the Nepali (Khas) language.
