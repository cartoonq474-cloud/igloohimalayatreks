Everest Base Camp Luxury Trek — 14 Days

Follow the Everest Base Camp trail with five-star stays in Kathmandu and premium lodges at selected lower Khumbu village stops. Our 14-day Everest Base Camp Luxury Trek visits Namche, Debuche and Dingboche before continuing to Base Camp and Kala Patthar. Two acclimatisation days allow time at the same sleeping altitude. Higher on the route, expect simpler lodges with shared bathrooms, cold nights and demanding uphill walking. Return through Pheriche and Namche before the final trek to Lukla. Our 14-day itinerary combines the classic Khumbu route with upgraded comfort where available. Stay two nights in a five-star Kathmandu hotel, five nights in premium lodges at Phakding, Namche Bazaar and Lukla, and six nights in traditional mountain lodges as you ascend through Dingboche and Lobuche to Gorak Shep, Everest Base Camp (5,364 m), and sunrise at Kala Patthar (5,545 m).

Luxury Base Camp Trek Overview

Our 14-day Everest Base Camp Luxury Trek combines the Khumbu route with upgraded stays where available. Two nights in a five-star Kathmandu hotel and five nights in premium lodges at Phakding, Namche and Lukla frame the higher trekking stages. You visit Everest Base Camp and hike to Kala Patthar before returning through Pheriche and Namche.

After flying to Lukla, follow the Dudh Koshi valley through Phakding, crossing suspension bridges before climbing to Namche Bazaar. Beyond Namche, the hillside trail descends to the river and climbs to Tengboche, known for its Buddhist monastery and views of Ama Dablam. Continue down through forest to Debuche for the night. Sherpa village homes, prayer wheels and mani walls reflect the Buddhist traditions of communities along the route.

As you gain altitude, forested paths give way to open slopes and increasingly rocky ground. You continue through Dingboche and Lobuche to Gorak Shep, your overnight base for visiting Everest Base Camp. The following day, you hike to Kala Patthar before descending to Pheriche. The return journey takes you through Namche and back to Lukla.

We include acclimatisation days in both Namche and Dingboche. These combine shorter hikes with another night at the same elevation, allowing time to adjust before continuing higher. You’ll still need to prepare for several consecutive walking days, steep climbs and uneven trails. Technical climbing skills are not required, but the altitude and sustained physical effort make this a demanding trek.

You stay in Kathmandu for two nights, premium lodges for five nights and simpler mountain lodges for six nights. The programme covers arrival, trekking, return travel and departure.

Luxury Base Camp Trek Itinerary — Day by Day

Our 14-day itinerary includes the journey from Kathmandu, two acclimatisation days and the return trek to Lukla. Walking times and distances are approximate; your pace, weather and trail conditions can affect each day’s schedule.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu

Arrival & Airport Transfer — Kathmandu, around 1,400 m — 5-Star Hotel Check-in

Main Activity Arrival and airport transfer
Overnight Altitude Approximately 1,400 m
Accommodation 5-star hotel in Kathmandu, twin sharing
Meals Breakfast, subject to arrival time; lunch and dinner at own expense

Welcome to Nepal. Our representative meets you at Tribhuvan International Airport and accompanies you to your hotel. After you settle in, we go through the trekking programme, check your essential equipment and discuss any questions you have.

Depending on your arrival time, you can explore the nearby streets of Thamel or rest after your flight. We also help with any final preparations before you leave for the Everest region.

Day 2: Fly to Lukla and Trek to Phakding

Mountain Flight & Valley Walk — Phakding, 2,610 m — ~3–4 hours, ~8 km

Walking Time Approximately 3–4 hours
Trek Distance Approximately 8 km
Overnight Altitude 2,610 m
Accommodation Premium lodge in Phakding
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Your mountain journey begins with a flight to Lukla at 2,860 m. Depending on airline operations and the season, the flight may depart from Kathmandu or Manthali Airport in Ramechhap.

After landing, you meet your trekking team and begin walking towards Phakding. The trail passes village homes, prayer wheels and forested slopes, with downhill stretches and shorter climbs along the way. Sections follow the Dudh Koshi River, introducing you to the valley you will explore over the coming days. We spend the night in Phakding.

Day 3: Trek from Phakding to Namche Bazaar

Dudh Koshi Valley & Sagarmatha Ascent — Namche Bazaar, 3,440 m — ~5–6 hours, ~12 km

Walking Time Approximately 5–6 hours
Trek Distance Approximately 12 km
Overnight Altitude 3,440 m
Accommodation Premium lodge in Namche Bazaar
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We follow the Dudh Koshi through villages and pine forests, passing Benkar and Monjo before entering Sagarmatha National Park. Your guide handles the permit checks along the route.

Suspension bridges connect the valley trails, followed by a sustained uphill walk towards Namche. This final climb is the most demanding part of the day, so we allow time for breaks. Namche’s shops, lodges and houses spread across the hillside, making it an important trading town and a useful place to spend the next two nights.

Day 4: Acclimatisation Day in Namche Bazaar

Acclimatisation Hike to Hotel Everest View — Namche Bazaar, 3,440 m — High Point: ~3,880 m

Walking Time Approximately 4–5 hours, adjusted to conditions and health
Hike Distance Approximately 7 km
Daytime High Point Around 3,880 m if visiting Hotel Everest View
Overnight Altitude 3,440 m
Accommodation Premium lodge in Namche Bazaar
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We stay another night in Namche and take a shorter hike above the town. A usual option is the trail towards Hotel Everest View, where clear weather offers views of Everest and nearby peaks.

Your guide chooses the walk according to conditions and how the group is feeling. Afterwards, we return to Namche for the night. The afternoon gives you time to rest, visit the shops or stop at a café before continuing towards Debuche tomorrow.

Day 5: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Debuche via Tengboche

Tengboche Monastery & Woodland Descent — Debuche, around 3,800 m — ~5–6 hours, ~12 km

Walking Time Approximately 5–6 hours
Trek Distance Approximately 12 km
Overnight Altitude Debuche, around 3,800 m
Accommodation Mountain lodge in Debuche, shared toilets and bathrooms
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Leaving Namche, we follow the hillside trail before descending towards Phunki Tenga. Mountain views accompany sections of the walk, while the river marks the low point before the climb to Tengboche.

The climb to Tengboche passes through pine and rhododendron forest. If the monastery is open, your guide explains visiting customs before you enter. We then descend through woodland to Debuche, where we spend the night in a mountain lodge. Your lodge provides a room with shared toilets and bathroom facilities.

Day 6: Trek from Debuche to Dingboche

Imja Khola Valley & Alpine Scrub — Dingboche, 4,410 m — ~5–6 hours, ~11 km

Walking Time Approximately 5–6 hours
Trek Distance Approximately 11 km
Overnight Altitude 4,410 m
Accommodation Mountain lodge in Dingboche, shared toilets and bathrooms
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

From Debuche, walk through forest and cross the Imja Khola before continuing through Pangboche. As the route gains height, the trees become fewer and the valley opens into slopes with low vegetation and stone buildings.

The trail passes Somare before reaching Dingboche in the Imja Valley. Today brings a noticeable gain in sleeping altitude, so we keep a steady pace throughout the walk. After checking into your teahouse, the remaining afternoon is available for rest before tomorrow’s acclimatisation hike.

Day 7: Acclimatisation Day in Dingboche — Hike to Chhukung

Imja Valley Hike towards Chhukung — Dingboche, 4,410 m — High Point: ~4,730 m

Walking Time Approximately 4–5 hours, adjusted to conditions and health
Hike Distance Approximately 10–11 km, including the return walk
Overnight Altitude 4,410 m
Accommodation Mountain lodge in Dingboche, shared toilets and bathrooms
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Our second acclimatisation day follows the Imja Valley towards Chhukung, at around 4,730 m. This is a walking day, with a return to Dingboche afterwards so you spend another night at the same sleeping elevation.

Open slopes and mountain views accompany the valley trail, including Lhotse’s south face in clear weather. Your guide adjusts the walking pace and turnaround to the group’s condition. We return to the lodge for rest before tomorrow’s walk towards Lobuche.

Day 8: Trek from Dingboche to Lobuche

Thukla Pass Memorials & Khumbu Moraine — Lobuche, around 4,910 m — ~5–6 hours, ~9 km

Walking Time Approximately 5–6 hours
Trek Distance Approximately 9 km
Overnight Altitude Around 4,910 m
Accommodation Mountain lodge in Lobuche, shared toilets and bathrooms
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Dingboche and cross open ground towards Thukla. After a break, the trail climbs more steeply to the memorial area above the village. The stone memorials commemorate climbers and Sherpas who lost their lives in the mountains.

Beyond this section, we continue beside the Khumbu Glacier towards Lobuche. Uneven ground and increasing altitude make the walking more demanding, even where the gradient eases. We settle into the teahouse and prepare for tomorrow’s journey to Everest Base Camp.

Day 9: Trek to Everest Base Camp and Return to Gorak Shep

Glacial Moraine & Base Camp Landmark — Gorak Shep, approx 5,164 m — Highest Point: 5,364 m

Walking Time Approximately 8–9 hours
Total Trek Distance Approximately 11–12 km
Highest Point Everest Base Camp, 5,364 m
Overnight Altitude Gorak Shep, approximately 5,164 m
Accommodation Mountain lodge in Gorak Shep, shared toilets and bathrooms
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We start from Lobuche and follow the rocky trail towards Gorak Shep. After a break, we continue to Everest Base Camp, crossing uneven glacial ground along the Khumbu Glacier.

Reaching base camp brings you into the landscape used by Everest expedition teams. During the spring climbing season, tents and expedition activity may be visible. At other times, the glacier and surrounding mountains define the visit.

After spending time at base camp, we retrace the trail to Gorak Shep. Tonight’s stop puts us close to the starting point for tomorrow’s Kala Patthar hike.

Day 10: Hike to Kala Patthar and Descend to Pheriche

Kala Patthar Viewpoint & Valley Descent — Pheriche, approx 4,240 m — Highest Point: ~5,545 m

Walking Time Approximately 7–8 hours for the hike and descent
Total Trek Distance Approximately 17 km
Highest Point Kala Patthar viewpoint, approximately 5,545 m
Overnight Altitude Pheriche, approximately 4,240 m
Accommodation Mountain lodge in Pheriche, shared toilets and bathrooms
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We make an early start for Kala Patthar. The climb above Gorak Shep is steep in places, and the altitude makes it demanding. In clear weather, the viewpoint offers close views of Everest, Nuptse, Pumori and the surrounding Khumbu peaks.

After the hike, we return to Gorak Shep for breakfast and continue towards Pheriche. The route passes back through Lobuche and the Thukla area before descending into the valley. It is another substantial walking day, ending with an overnight stay at a lower elevation.

Day 11: Trek from Pheriche to Namche Bazaar

Downhill Valley Walk & Hillside Traverse — Namche Bazaar, 3,440 m — ~6–8 hours, ~20 km

Walking Time Approximately 6–8 hours, depending on pace
Trek Distance Approximately 20 km
Overnight Altitude 3,440 m
Accommodation Premium lodge in Namche Bazaar
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We continue down the valley through Pangboche and return towards Tengboche. Forests gradually reappear as we lose altitude, bringing a change from the rocky terrain of the upper route.

The day combines long descents with an uphill section after crossing the river near Phunki Tenga. From there, we follow the hillside towards Namche. Although we finish much lower than we started, this remains a long walking day. Once back in town, you have time to rest at the lodge.

Day 12: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Lukla

Final Suspension Bridges & Valley Trail — Lukla, 2,860 m — ~6–7 hours, ~20 km

Walking Time Approximately 6–7 hours
Trek Distance Approximately 20 km
Overnight Altitude 2,860 m
Accommodation Premium lodge in Lukla
Meals Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Our final trekking day follows the route down from Namche through Jorsalle, Monjo and Phakding. You cross familiar suspension bridges and return through the forested sections of the Dudh Koshi valley.

The approach to Lukla includes uphill stretches, so there is still some effort ahead after the main descent. On arrival, we check into the lodge for the last night in the mountains. The evening gives you time to organise your belongings and spend time with your guide and porter team.

Day 13: Fly from Lukla and Return to Kathmandu

Scenic Mountain Flight & Farewell Celebration — Kathmandu, approx 1,400 m — 5-Star Hotel Stay

Main Activity Flight from Lukla and onward travel to Kathmandu
Overnight Altitude Approximately 1,400 m
Accommodation 5-star hotel in Kathmandu, twin sharing
Meals Breakfast and farewell dinner

We prepare for the return flight from Lukla. Depending on the operating schedule, you may fly directly to Kathmandu or land at Manthali Airport and continue by road.

After reaching your hotel, the remaining time is free for rest, shopping or exploring nearby areas. We share a farewell dinner in Kathmandu, bringing the trekking programme to a close. Flight timing remains dependent on weather and airline operations.

Day 14: Departure from Kathmandu

Hotel Checkout & Private Airport Transfer — Onward International Departure

Main Activity Departure from Nepal
Meals Breakfast

Your 14-day Luxury Base Camp Trek ends today. After breakfast, you prepare for your onward journey. Depending on your departure time, there may be an opportunity for a final walk nearby before leaving the hotel.

Our representative accompanies you to Tribhuvan International Airport for your departure.

Domestic flights between Kathmandu or Manthali and Lukla enforce a strict baggage allowance of 15 kg (33 lbs) per passenger. This includes 10 kg for your main duffel bag (carried by your porter) and 5 kg for your daypack. Excess non-trekking luggage can be stored securely and free of charge at your 5-star hotel in Kathmandu until your return.

1. Trek Difficulty and Fitness

Physically demanding high-altitude trekking; 5–6 to 8–9 hours daily; endurance prep required.

The Luxury Base Camp Trek is physically demanding, although it does not require technical climbing skills. You will walk over steep paths, stone steps and uneven ground on consecutive days. Several stages take five to six hours, while longer days can reach eight to nine hours.

Previous hiking experience is helpful. First-time trekkers should build their endurance before departure and become comfortable walking uphill and downhill with a daypack.

Begin preparing around two to three months beforehand, allowing longer if you are starting from a low fitness level. Gradually combine regular walks, hill hikes and strength exercises. Practise in the boots you will wear on the trek.

2. Altitude and Acclimatisation

Scheduled acclimatisation in Namche & Dingboche; report symptoms immediately; safety first.

Our itinerary includes acclimatisation days in Namche Bazaar and Dingboche. These give you time to adjust before continuing higher, but altitude illness can still affect you, regardless of fitness.

Tell your guide immediately about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not continue to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite resting at the same elevation, descent is necessary. Reaching Base Camp must remain secondary to your health.

3. Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

Mandatory coverage up to 5,545 m including helicopter evacuation; consult doctor beforehand.

If you have an existing medical condition, seek advice from a healthcare professional familiar with high-altitude travel before booking. Discuss your planned route and any medicines you take.

Travel insurance is compulsory for this package. Confirm that your policy covers trekking to approximately 5,545 metres, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment in Nepal. Check exclusions, excess charges and the insurer’s emergency assistance procedure. Carry your policy details and emergency contact number.

4. Accommodation and Room Facilities

5-star Kathmandu, 5 nights premium lodges; shared bathrooms on higher stages.

Your accommodation includes two nights in a five-star Kathmandu hotel, five nights in premium lodges and six nights in simpler mountain lodges. Upgraded facilities are available at selected lower trekking stops.

Debuche, Dingboche, Lobuche, Gorak Shep and Pheriche use lodges with shared toilets and bathrooms. Expect cold nights and fewer facilities higher on the route. Ask us about individual room arrangements, bedding and services before confirming your booking.

5. Food and Dietary Requirements

Full board trekking meals, fresh fruit, hot drink per meal; inform us of allergies in advance.

Trekking includes breakfast, lunch and dinner, with seasonal fruit and one hot drink per meal. Kathmandu includes breakfast and a farewell dinner.

Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before booking so we can discuss what can be arranged along the route. Explain any strict requirements clearly, including concerns about shared cooking equipment.

6. Drinking Water

Reusable bottles; water-purification tablets provided; boil or disinfect all tap water.

Carry reusable bottles. We provide water-purification tablets; follow the instructions. Use boiled or appropriately treated water when filling your bottles from lodge taps.

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs. If boiling is unavailable, filtration followed by disinfection offers broader protection than many portable filters alone. Follow the instructions for your treatment equipment or tablets.

7. Guide and Porter Support

Licensed guide and 1 porter per 2 trekkers; full wages, meals, and insurance included.

A government-licensed, English-speaking guide leads the trek, with one porter shared between two trekkers. Staff wages, meals, insurance and equipment are included. Your porter carries the main luggage while you carry your daypack.

8. Showers, Charging and Communication

Premium amenities at lower stops; varying charges higher up; power banks recommended.

Premium lodges provide more facilities at selected lower stops. Showers, Wi-Fi and charging arrangements vary, particularly higher on the route. Ask us to confirm included services and charges for your lodges. Mobile reception can be unreliable; carry a power bank and allow for periods without contact.

9. Permits and Documents

Sagarmatha National Park, Khumbu entry permission, and TIMS documentation fully arranged.

We arrange Sagarmatha National Park entry, Khumbu local entry permission and TIMS documentation for the trek. Carry your passport, insurance details and copies of important documents. Your Nepal visa and additional personal documentation remain your responsibility.

10. Flight Delays and Extra Time

Lukla weather contingency; Manthali alternative routing; allow buffer days before departure.

Lukla flights can be delayed or cancelled because of weather. During peak periods, flights may use Manthali Airport in Ramechhap, requiring a road transfer.

Allow extra time before your international departure. Additional accommodation and alternative transport may cost extra. Helicopter availability also depends on weather and operating conditions.

Luxury Base Camp Trek FAQs

General & Route

Can I do this trek without previous trekking experience? ∨

Yes, provided you prepare well and have suitable fitness. Previous hiking experience helps, but technical climbing skills are unnecessary. Before departure, practise longer hill walks with a daypack and become comfortable walking uphill and downhill on consecutive days.

What is the highest altitude on this itinerary? ∨

The highest planned point is Kala Patthar at approximately 5,545 metres, above Everest Base Camp at 5,364 metres. Your highest overnight stop is Gorak Shep at approximately 5,164 metres, where you stay after visiting Base Camp.

Can I see Mount Everest from Base Camp? ∨

Everest’s summit is generally hidden behind nearby ridges from Base Camp itself. Kala Patthar provides the clearer view of Everest that many travellers expect. Our itinerary includes this viewpoint, although mountain visibility depends on the weather.

Do we sleep at Everest Base Camp? ∨

No. You visit Base Camp during the day and return to a teahouse in Gorak Shep for the night. The following morning includes the planned Kala Patthar hike before descending towards Pheriche. Camping at Base Camp is not included.

Are acclimatisation days complete rest days? ∨

They include a hike towards Hotel Everest View from Namche or towards Chhukung from Dingboche, followed by another night in the same village. Your guide adjusts the pace and turnaround according to conditions and health.

Does being fit protect me from altitude sickness? ∨

No. Good fitness helps with the walking demands, but it does not prevent altitude illness. Fit and experienced trekkers can also become unwell. Follow the acclimatisation schedule and report symptoms immediately instead of assuming fitness will protect you.

What should I do if I feel unwell at altitude? ∨

Tell your guide immediately about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not ascend to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite rest at the same elevation, descent is necessary.

Is insurance covering trekking to 5,500 metres sufficient? ∨

A limit of 5,500 metres falls below the planned Kala Patthar elevation of approximately 5,545 metres. Ask your insurer to confirm coverage for the full route, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment, including any relevant exclusions or conditions.

Is luxury accommodation provided at every overnight trekking stop? ∨

No. The package includes two five-star hotel nights in Kathmandu and five nights in premium lodges in Phakding, Namche and Lukla. Debuche and the higher stops use simpler lodges with shared bathrooms. Confirm properties and facilities when discussing your dates.

Do I need to carry my main trekking bag? ∨

A porter carries your main trekking luggage, with one porter shared between two trekkers. You carry your daypack containing water, weather protection, medicines and other daily essentials. Keep your passport, money and valuables with you.

Do I need to bring a sleeping bag and down jacket? ∨

We provide a sleeping bag, down jacket and duffel bag for use during your trek. Return all three items afterwards. You remain responsible for equipment such as trekking boots, clothing, toiletries and electronics. Confirm suitability before departure.

Can I book as a solo traveller? ∨

Yes. You can enquire about joining a group departure or arranging a private trek. Send us your preferred dates so we can check the available options and confirm the price that applies to your booking.
