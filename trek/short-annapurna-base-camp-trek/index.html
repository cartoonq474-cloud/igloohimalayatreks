Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek Overview

Our Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek reaches the Sanctuary in nine days, including arrival, departure and travel through Pokhara. The walking route starts around Jhinu Danda and continues through Sinuwa and Deurali to ABC at 4,130 m. It suits travellers who can prepare for sustained uphill walking and have fewer days available for the journey.

After arriving in Kathmandu, you travel to Pokhara by tourist bus, then take a jeep towards Jhinu Danda. The first walk climbs through Chhomrong before crossing its river and continuing to Sinuwa. Beyond the villages, you follow the Modi Khola valley through Bamboo, Dovan and Himalaya. Forest paths, river crossings and steep stone steps make up much of the approach, with teahouses providing overnight stops along the route.

From Deurali, the route climbs through mountain terrain to Machhapuchhre Base Camp at 3,700 m. You continue up the valley to Annapurna Base Camp, surrounded by Annapurna I, Annapurna South and Hiunchuli. We spend a night at ABC before retracing the trail through Deurali and Himalaya to Bamboo, our final overnight stop on the trail.

The shorter route leaves out Ghorepani and Poon Hill, keeping the focus on the Sanctuary. There are no dedicated acclimatisation days, and the climb from Sinuwa through Deurali to ABC gains considerable sleeping altitude on consecutive nights. Stone staircases and long walking days require preparation. Discuss a slower ascent before booking if you need more time to adjust to altitude.

You stay in city hotels in Kathmandu and Pokhara, with four teahouse nights during the trek. The nine-day programme covers your arrival, walking journey, return travel and departure.

Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek Itinerary — Day by Day

Our nine-day itinerary includes travel from Kathmandu through Pokhara and five walking days to Annapurna Base Camp and back. Times and distances are approximate; weather, road conditions and pace can affect each day’s schedule.

Day 1: Arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, Kathmandu

Arrival & Airport Transfer — Kathmandu (1,400 m) — Welcome Briefing & Gear Check

OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m
ACCOMMODATION 3-star hotel in Kathmandu
MEALS Not included

Welcome to Nepal. Our representative meets you at Tribhuvan International Airport and transfers you to your hotel in Kathmandu. After checking in, you can rest and receive an introduction to the upcoming nine-day Annapurna Base Camp programme.

Depending on your arrival time, you can explore the nearby streets of Thamel or rest after your flight. This is also a chance to discuss questions before leaving for the Annapurna region.

Day 2: Drive from Kathmandu to Pokhara

Scenic Highway Drive — Pokhara (850 m) — ~7 Hours Scenic Tourist Bus via Prithvi Highway

DRIVING TIME Approximately 7 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions
ROAD DISTANCE Approximately 203 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 850 m
ACCOMMODATION 3-star hotel in Pokhara
MEALS Breakfast

After breakfast, we leave Kathmandu for Pokhara by tourist bus. The Prithvi Highway follows river valleys through the surrounding hills, with small towns and terraced farmland along the journey west.

The drive usually takes around seven hours, although roadworks and traffic can extend the journey. On arrival, you check into your hotel and have time to rest or walk near Phewa Lake. Clear weather may reveal Machhapuchhre beyond the hills. Prepare your trekking bag for tomorrow’s drive towards Jhinu Danda and the first walking stage.

Day 3: Drive to Jhinu Danda and Trek to Sinuwa

Jeep Drive & Valley Trek — Sinuwa (2,340 m) — ~2–3 Hrs Drive + 5–6 Hrs Trek (5.3 km) via Chhomrong

DRIVING TIME Approximately 2–3 hours
WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 5.3 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Sinuwa, around 2,340 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Sinuwa
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Pokhara by jeep and drive towards Jhinu Danda. After reaching the trail, we cross a suspension bridge and begin the steady uphill walk towards Chhomrong village.

Chhomrong is a Gurung village spread across the hillside, with mountain views when the weather is clear. The route descends on stone steps to the Chhomrong Khola before climbing again towards Sinuwa. This first stage involves sustained climbs and descents, so take breaks and keep a manageable pace. We spend the night in a local teahouse.

Day 4: Trek from Sinuwa to Deurali

Bamboo & Rhododendron Forest Ascent — Deurali (3,200 m) — ~6–7 Hrs Trek (12 km) along Modi Khola Valley

WALKING TIME Approximately 6–7 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 12 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 3,200 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Deurali
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Sinuwa and enter the forest, descending towards Bamboo before climbing up the Modi Khola valley. The shaded path passes rhododendron and bamboo, with river crossings and shorter stair sections between the scattered groups of mountain teahouses.

Beyond Dovan, we continue through Himalaya towards Deurali. The forest thins and the valley becomes open as we gain altitude. This is a long climbing day, ending at a teahouse around 3,200 m; tell your guide if you feel unwell.

Day 5: Trek from Deurali to Annapurna Base Camp via MBC

Sanctuary Ascent via MBC — Annapurna Base Camp (4,130 m) — ~4–5 Hrs Trek (5.2 km) into the Sanctuary Basin

WALKING TIME Approximately 4–5 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 5.2 km
MAIN STOP Machhapuchhre Base Camp, around 3,700 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Annapurna Base Camp, 4,130 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse at Annapurna Base Camp
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

From Deurali, we follow the upper valley towards Machhapuchhre Base Camp at 3,700 m. Forest gives way to open slopes, with snow or ice possible on the higher sections.

After a break at MBC, we continue towards Annapurna Base Camp. Annapurna I, Annapurna South and Hiunchuli stand around the Sanctuary, with Machhapuchhre behind us. The climb feels more demanding as the air gradually becomes thinner.

We stay in a teahouse at ABC, our highest overnight stop. The sleeping altitude rises about 930 m today; report symptoms promptly and follow your guide’s advice.

Day 6: Trek from Annapurna Base Camp to Bamboo

Sunrise Mountain Views & Descent — Bamboo (2,310 m) — ~6–7 Hrs Trek (18.7 km) Retracing via MBC & Deurali

WALKING TIME Approximately 6–7 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 18.7 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 2,310 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Bamboo
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

In clear weather, the morning offers views of the peaks around base camp. After breakfast, we retrace the upper valley through Machhapuchhre Base Camp and begin the long descent.

The route passes Deurali, Himalaya and Dovan before reaching Bamboo. As you lose altitude, open slopes give way to forest and the air usually feels warmer. The long downhill walk still requires attention on steps and uneven ground. Trekking poles can help with balance before we settle into our teahouse.

Day 7: Trek from Bamboo to Jhinu Danda and Drive to Pokhara

Trail Descent & Jeep Return — Pokhara (850 m) — ~5–6 Hrs Trek (11.6 km) via Jhinu + 2–3 Hrs Jeep Drive

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours, excluding any hot-spring visit
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 11.6 km
DRIVING TIME Approximately 2–3 hours, depending on road access and conditions
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Pokhara, around 850 m
ACCOMMODATION 3-star hotel in Pokhara
MEALS Breakfast and lunch; dinner in Pokhara not included

We walk from Bamboo towards Sinuwa, descend to the Chhomrong Khola and climb the stone staircase back to Chhomrong. From the village, the route drops towards Jhinu Danda and the road.

An optional hot-spring visit depends on access, conditions and the time available before our drive. The jeep journey returns us to Pokhara, where you check into your hotel after five days of walking. You can rest or walk near Phewa Lake. Dinner in Pokhara is at your own expense tonight.

Day 8: Drive to Kathmandu by Tourist Bus

Return Scenic Tourist Bus — Kathmandu (1,400 m) — ~7 Hours Scenic Drive (203 km) to Capital

DRIVING TIME Approximately 7 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions
ROAD DISTANCE Approximately 203 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m
ACCOMMODATION 3-star hotel in Kathmandu
MEALS Breakfast

After breakfast, we return from Pokhara to Kathmandu by tourist bus. The road follows river valleys through the hills; traffic and road conditions can affect the travel time.

The journey usually takes around seven hours, with stops along the way. On arrival, you check into your hotel and have the remaining time to rest, organise your belongings or explore nearby streets before tomorrow’s airport transfer and departure.

Hotel Breakfast & Departure — Kathmandu (1,400 m) — Airport Transfer & Onward Flight

Your Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek ends today. After breakfast, you prepare for your onward journey. Depending on your flight time, you may have an opportunity for a final walk nearby before leaving the hotel.

Trek Difficulty and Fitness

The Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek does not require technical climbing skills, but the walking can be demanding. You climb stone staircases and uneven paths on consecutive days. Longer stages take six to seven hours, and the compact ascent leaves limited time to adjust to altitude.

Previous hiking experience is helpful. First-time trekkers should build their endurance before departure and become comfortable walking uphill and downhill with a daypack.

Begin preparing around two to three months beforehand, allowing longer if you are starting from a low fitness level. Gradually combine regular walks, hill hikes and strength exercises. Practise in the boots you will wear on the trek.

Altitude and Acclimatisation

There are no dedicated acclimatisation days in this itinerary. Sleeping altitude rises around 860 m from Sinuwa to Deurali, then another 930 m to ABC on the following day.

Tell your guide promptly about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not continue to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite resting at the same elevation, descent is necessary. Reaching Base Camp must remain secondary to your health.

Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

If you have an existing medical condition, seek advice from a healthcare professional familiar with high-altitude travel before booking. Discuss your planned route and any medicines you take.

Arrange travel insurance for this package. Confirm that your policy covers trekking to at least 4,130 metres, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment in Nepal. Check exclusions, excess charges and the insurer’s emergency assistance procedure. Carry your policy details and emergency contact number.

Accommodation and Room Facilities

You stay in three-star hotels in Kathmandu and Pokhara, with breakfast included, and teahouses during the trek. City hotels offer more facilities, while mountain rooms become simpler as you climb higher.

At Deurali and ABC, expect basic rooms and shared bathroom facilities. Private rooms can be limited during peak periods, and sharing may be necessary. Prepare for cold nights at Annapurna Base Camp, even in spring and autumn.

Food and Dietary Requirements

Trekking meals are included; dinner in Pokhara on Day 7 costs extra. Lodge menus commonly offer dal bhat, noodles, potatoes and soups.

Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before booking so we can discuss what can be arranged along the route. Explain any strict requirements clearly, including concerns about shared cooking equipment.

Drinking Water

Carry reusable bottles and drink boiled or appropriately treated water. Water from a clear stream or an untreated tap may still contain germs.

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs. If boiling is unavailable, filtration followed by disinfection offers broader protection than many portable filters alone. Follow the instructions for your treatment equipment or tablets.

Guide and Porter Support

An experienced trekking guide is included. Optional porter hire costs USD 35 per day and may be shared by two trekkers. Without a porter, you carry your main bag and your daily essentials.

Showers, Charging and Communication

Hot showers, Wi-Fi and device charging cost extra, with availability varying between lodges. Mobile reception and internet connections can be unreliable, particularly higher on the route. Carry a power bank and let family know that you may be out of contact for periods of the trek.

Permits and Documents

The ACAP entry permit is included. Ask us about TIMS registration arrangements before your departure. Carry your passport, insurance details and copies of important documents. Your Nepal visa remains your responsibility, separate from the trekking package.

Road Conditions and Extra Time

Roadworks, traffic and heavy rain can delay travel between Kathmandu and Pokhara. Road access also affects the jeep journeys to and from Jhinu Danda.

Allow extra time before your international departure. Accommodation and transport outside the programme may cost extra if road delays or itinerary changes affect travel.

Weather and Best Time for the Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek

Spring, from March to May, and autumn, from September to November, are generally the best seasons for our short ABC trekking route. Both offer favourable walking conditions and opportunities for mountain views, although rain, cloud, cold weather and snowfall remain possible along the route.

Seasonal Weather Conditions

4 Seasons Matrix
Season Months What to Expect
Spring March–May Days gradually become warmer, with rhododendrons flowering along suitable forest sections. March can still bring snow higher on the route. April often balances walking temperatures and visibility, while May can bring afternoon cloud or rain.
Autumn September–November Early September can retain monsoon rain. October and November generally offer clearer mountain views after the rains, with cooler mornings and increasingly cold nights around base camp. Popular weeks can be busy.
Winter December–February Clear days and quieter trails are possible, but cold, shorter daylight hours, snow and ice make the upper route more demanding. Heavy snowfall and avalanche conditions can affect access above Deurali.
Monsoon June–August, sometimes extending into September Frequent rain makes lower trails wet and slippery, with leeches possible in forest sections. Cloud can hide the mountains, while landslides or flooding may affect roads and trails. Travel plans need flexibility.

These are seasonal patterns; conditions can vary from year to year and during an individual trek.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Elevation & Microclimates

Walking from Jhinu Danda through Chhomrong and Sinuwa can feel relatively warm in sunshine. Conditions turn colder around Deurali and ABC, especially in the early mornings and evenings after sunset.

Even during spring and autumn, nights at Annapurna Base Camp can fall below freezing. Wind, cloud and damp clothing can make stops feel colder. Carry warm layers and weather protection whichever month you choose, and follow your guide’s advice.

Which Season Should You Choose?

Trekker Timing Advice

Choose spring if gradually warming days and flowering forests interest you alongside the base camp journey. Choose autumn, particularly October or November, if mountain visibility is your main priority. Winter and monsoon require greater flexibility and preparation for demanding conditions.

Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek FAQs

Clear answers to the most common questions about the Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek, direct route walking, accommodation, and preparation.

General & Route

Can I do this trek without previous trekking experience? ∨
Previous hiking experience helps because the ascent is compact and several days are demanding. First-time trekkers should prepare with consecutive hill walks and discuss fitness and altitude concerns before booking. Technical climbing skills are unnecessary on this route.
What is the highest altitude on this itinerary? ∨
Annapurna Base Camp at approximately 4,130 metres is the highest planned point and overnight stop. You pass Machhapuchhre Base Camp at around 3,700 metres on the way from Deurali before continuing along the valley to ABC.
Does this short route include Ghorepani and Poon Hill? ∨
No. This short itinerary uses the direct approach from Jhinu Danda through Sinuwa and Deurali. Ghorepani and Poon Hill feature in our longer ABC package, which includes the sunrise hike and additional village and forest walking.
Do we sleep at Annapurna Base Camp? ∨
Yes. You stay in a teahouse at Annapurna Base Camp on Day 5, then descend towards Bamboo the following day. Camping is not part of this programme. Expect basic facilities, cold nights and possible room sharing during busy periods.
Are there dedicated acclimatisation days? ∨
No. You climb from Sinuwa to Deurali, then sleep at ABC the following night, with substantial altitude gains. Discuss a longer itinerary and extra acclimatisation time before booking if you have concerns about the ascent.
Is insurance covering trekking to 4,000 metres sufficient? ∨
No. A limit of 4,000 metres falls below Annapurna Base Camp at approximately 4,130 metres. Ask your insurer to confirm coverage for the full route, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment, including relevant exclusions, policy limits and conditions.
Does being fit protect me from altitude sickness? ∨
No. Good fitness helps with the walking demands, but it does not prevent altitude illness. Fit and experienced trekkers can also become unwell. Follow your guide’s advice and report symptoms promptly instead of assuming fitness will protect you.
What should I do if I feel unwell at altitude? ∨
Tell your guide promptly about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not ascend to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite rest at the same elevation, descent is necessary.
Do I need to carry my main trekking bag? ∨
Yes, unless you hire a porter. Porter service costs USD 35 per day and may be shared by two trekkers. With a porter, you carry a daypack; keep your medicines, water and valuables with you.
Do I need to bring a sleeping bag and down jacket? ∨
We provide a sleeping bag and down jacket for use during the trek. Bring your own main bag, boots, clothing, toiletries and electronics. Ask us about the supplied equipment before departure if you have particular comfort requirements.
Can I book as a solo traveller? ∨
Yes. You can enquire about joining a group departure or arranging a private trek. Send us your preferred dates so we can check the available options and confirm the price that applies to your booking.
How does the 13-day ABC package differ from this itinerary? ∨
Our Annapurna Base Camp Trek via Poon Hill — 13 Days adds Ghorepani and the Poon Hill sunrise hike before entering the Sanctuary. This nine-day itinerary takes a shorter approach from Jhinu Danda, with fewer days and a compact ascent.
