North Annapurna Base Camp Trek — 9 Days

Explore Annapurna’s northern side on a nine-day camping trek from Humkhola to North Annapurna Base Camp. Walk via Sidikharka, Buket Mela and the Panchakunda lake area, named Panch Pokhari in this itinerary, before reaching the base camp viewpoint at approximately 4,190 metres. Return along the same mountain trail, with road journeys via Beni and Pokhara. Expect steep paths, cold camping nights and limited facilities. An English-speaking guide, porter support and ACAP permit are included in the arrangements.

North ABC Camping Trek Overview

Our nine-day North Annapurna Base Camp Trek follows a quieter approach on Annapurna's northern side. Travel to Beni, continue by jeep to Humkhola and walk via Sidikharka, Buket Mela and Panch Pokhari. Visit the planned base camp viewpoint before returning to Sidikharka. The journey finishes with road travel to Pokhara, then Kathmandu, followed by departure.

This northern route follows the approach associated with Maurice Herzog's 1950 Annapurna journey. It lies in Myagdi, away from the established southern sanctuary trail. Forest paths above Humkhola lead towards higher grazing areas, rocky ground and the Panchakunda lake area. Mountain facilities are limited, so the walking section uses camping arrangements. Prepare for steep climbs, uneven paths, stream crossings and weather that can change the day's walking plan.

Panch Pokhari refers here to the Panchakunda lake area near North Annapurna Base Camp. You camp in this high area before the base camp hike, reaching approximately 4,190 metres during the day. Annapurna I rises above the northern valley, with snow, ice and rocky slopes shaping the surrounding landscape. Mountain visibility depends on the weather.

The return follows the same mountain corridor, passing Panch Pokhari and Buket Mela before the night at Sidikharka. Next, descend to Humkhola and drive to Pokhara, allowing for rough road conditions and a long combined travel day. The following day's tourist bus returns to Kathmandu. No domestic flights are included in this nine-day itinerary, which finishes with departure from Nepal.

The trek suits travellers prepared for camping, limited communication and sustained walking at altitude. Discuss your experience, equipment and the rapid ascent from Beni before confirming your booking.

North ABC Camping Trek Itinerary — Day by Day

Our nine-day itinerary follows the northern approach from Humkhola to North Annapurna Base Camp, returning along the same trail. Walking and driving times are estimates; weather, road access, trail conditions and pace affect arrangements.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu

Arrival & Briefing — Kathmandu, approximately 1,400 m / 4,593 ft — Hotel Check-in

MAIN ACTIVITY Arrival, hotel transfer arrangements and trek briefing
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m / 4,593 ft
ACCOMMODATION Hotel in Kathmandu; category and room sharing as quoted
MEALS City meals as confirmed in your quotation

Welcome to Nepal. After arriving at Tribhuvan International Airport, follow the agreed transfer arrangements to your Kathmandu hotel. We discuss the northern Annapurna route, check equipment and review the camping preparations before you leave the city tomorrow.

Depending on arrival time, rest or collect any remaining personal items. Keep your passport, insurance information and permit documents ready, and confirm the departure arrangements for tomorrow's road journey west to Beni.

Day 2: Drive from Kathmandu to Beni

Scenic Hill Highway Drive — Beni, approximately 830 m / 2,723 ft — 9–11 hrs

DRIVING TIME Approximately 9-11 hours; traffic, road works and stops can extend the journey
MAIN ROUTE Kathmandu — central hill highway — Beni
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 830 m / 2,723 ft
ACCOMMODATION Local hotel or lodge in Beni; arrangements as quoted
MEALS Road-day and Beni meals as confirmed in your quotation

We leave Kathmandu by road and travel west towards Beni, the Myagdi town used for access to northern Annapurna. River valleys, cultivated hillsides and towns accompany the journey.

This is a full travel day, with arrival depending on traffic and road conditions. After reaching Beni, rest and prepare your daypack. Confirm the jeep departure, current access to Humkhola and the arrangements for tomorrow's first mountain camping night.

Day 3: Jeep to Humkhola and Trek to Sidikharka

Jeep Trailhead & Valley Ascent — Sidikharka, approximately 3,160 m / 10,367 ft — 6–8 hrs combined

DRIVING TIME Approximately 3-4 hours from Beni, subject to jeep access and road conditions
WALKING TIME Approximately 3-4 hours from Humkhola; route conditions can change timing
TRAILHEAD ALTITUDE Humkhola, approximately 2,890 m / 9,482 ft
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Sidikharka, approximately 3,160 m / 10,367 ft
ACCOMMODATION Tent camp at Sidikharka; exact site confirmed locally
MEALS Camp meals as quoted; seasonal fresh fruit after dinner

A jeep takes us from Beni towards Humkhola via the Tatopani and Narchyang area. Humkhola marks the start of the walking route, already high above Beni's much lower overnight elevation.

From Humkhola, follow the mountain trail towards Sidikharka, also written Sandhikharka. Forest paths, uphill sections and stream crossings require steady footing. The combined drive and walk brings a large sleeping-altitude gain, so report headaches, nausea or unusual fatigue immediately. Reach camp, check your sleeping equipment and keep the afternoon gentle while the team prepares dinner.

Day 4: Trek from Sidikharka to Buket Mela

Forest & Alpine Grazing Trail — Buket Mela, approximately 3,551 m / 11,650 ft — 3–4 hrs / ~6 km

WALKING TIME Approximately 3-4 hours, depending on the trail and group pace
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 6 km; exact campsite and route can change this
MAIN ROUTE Sidikharka — Guphaphant area — Buket Mela
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 3,551 m / 11,650 ft
ACCOMMODATION Tent camp at Buket Mela; site subject to local conditions
MEALS Camp meals as quoted; seasonal fresh fruit after dinner

We leave Sidikharka and continue towards Buket Mela, also written Bhusket Mela. The path gains height across grazing areas and rocky ground as the forest begins to thin.

Keep a steady pace and allow time for regular breaks rather than rushing to camp. Today's sleeping height rises by about 390 metres. Views towards the surrounding mountains depend on cloud and visibility. On arrival, settle into the tents, keep warm clothing accessible and discuss how you feel before tomorrow's climb towards the higher lake area.

Day 5: Trek from Buket Mela to Panch Pokhari

Alpine Ascent to Lake High Camp — Panch Pokhari, approximately 4,050 m / 13,287 ft — 4–5 hrs / 5–6 km

WALKING TIME Approximately 4-5 hours; weather and trail conditions affect timing
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 5-6 km, depending on the campsite and selected path
MAIN DESTINATION Panchakunda lake area, named Panch Pokhari in this itinerary
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 4,050 m / 13,287 ft
ACCOMMODATION Tent camp in the Panchakunda lake area; site confirmed locally
MEALS Camp meals as quoted; seasonal fresh fruit after dinner

We climb from Buket Mela towards Panch Pokhari, the Panchakunda lake area. Higher rocky ground replaces the lower forest, with mountain views when cloud allows before we reach the planned camp.

The planned camp stands around 4,050 metres, approximately 500 metres above last night's stop. Walk steadily, drink treated water and keep the guide informed about any symptoms. Choose a gentle afternoon around camp rather than adding unnecessary climbs. Cold, wind and changing cloud can affect comfort beside the high lake.

Day 6: Hike to North Annapurna Base Camp, Return via Panch Pokhari to Sidikharka

Northern Base Camp Viewpoint — Base Camp, ~4,190 m / 13,747 ft → Sidikharka, ~3,160 m — 6–8 hrs

WALKING TIME Approximately 6-8 hours; the selected viewpoint, conditions and group pace affect timing
HIGHEST PLANNED POINT North Annapurna Base Camp, approximately 4,190 m / 13,747 ft
RETURN ROUTE Base camp — Panch Pokhari — Buket Mela area — Sidikharka
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 3,160 m / 10,367 ft
ACCOMMODATION Tent camp at Sidikharka; no overnight stay at base camp
MEALS Camp meals as quoted; seasonal fresh fruit after dinner

We start early for the hike from the lake area towards North Annapurna Base Camp. The turnaround is approximately 4,190 metres, beneath the northern side of Annapurna I. Snow, visibility and trail conditions determine how far the group can continue.

After time at the viewpoint, carefully retrace the route via Panch Pokhari and descend past Buket Mela towards Sidikharka. This is a long walking day, with loose stones and uneven ground requiring care. Carry sufficient water and confirm a packed lunch before leaving camp.

Day 7: Trek to Humkhola and Drive to Pokhara

Trailhead Descent & Lakeside Drive — Pokhara, approximately 822 m / 2,697 ft — 8–11 hrs combined

WALKING TIME Approximately 3-4 hours to Humkhola
DRIVING TIME Approximately 5-7 hours onward to Pokhara; rough roads and delays can extend this
MAIN ROUTE Sidikharka — Humkhola; road journey via Narchyang, Tatopani and Beni
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 822 m / 2,697 ft
ACCOMMODATION Hotel in Pokhara; category and room sharing as quoted
MEALS Camp breakfast; remaining road-day and Pokhara meals as quoted

Leave Sidikharka and descend along the approach trail towards Humkhola. Take care on steep or wet sections, especially where paths cross streams. The final walking stage of the day ends at the agreed vehicle pickup point near Humkhola.

From Humkhola, drive via Narchyang, Tatopani and Beni to Pokhara. Walking and driving make this a long day, with timing dependent on current road conditions. Keep the evening flexible, check in at your hotel and rest after the mountain section.

Day 8: Tourist Bus from Pokhara to Kathmandu

Scenic Highway Return — Kathmandu, approximately 1,400 m / 4,593 ft — 7–9 hrs / ~200 km

DRIVING TIME Approximately 7-9 hours; traffic and road conditions can cause delays
ROAD DISTANCE Approximately 200 km, depending on pickup and routing
MAIN ACTIVITY Tourist bus journey from Pokhara to Kathmandu
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m / 4,593 ft
ACCOMMODATION Hotel in Kathmandu; category and room sharing as quoted
MEALS City and road-day meals as confirmed in your quotation

After the pickup arrangements, board the tourist bus for Kathmandu. The road journey follows river valleys and hill towns, with stops along the way before reaching the capital.

Road works, traffic and weather can extend the journey, so keep the day flexible. Once in Kathmandu, transfer to your hotel according to the agreed arrangements. Check tomorrow's departure time and prepare your documents and luggage for the airport.

Day 9: Departure from Kathmandu

Final Transfer — Homeward Bound — Kathmandu Departure

MAIN ACTIVITY Final departure and agreed airport transfer arrangements
MEALS Departure-day meals as confirmed in your quotation

Your nine-day North Annapurna Base Camp Trek ends today. Prepare for your journey and confirm the airport transfer time. Depending on your flight schedule, there may be time to rest or make purchases before departure.

Follow the agreed transfer arrangements to Tribhuvan International Airport for departure.

Read Before You Book

Before booking the North Annapurna Base Camp Trek, consider the altitude gain, camping conditions and limited facilities. These details help you assess the nine-day route against your experience, fitness and travel preferences.

Trek Difficulty and Fitness

This is a challenging trek with steep climbs, uneven paths, stream crossings and cold camping nights. The base camp return day involves several hours of ascent and descent. Limited facilities and difficult road access add practical demands, so previous hill walking and preparation are strongly recommended.

Practise consecutive hill walks with a daypack, including descents and uneven ground. Camping experience helps you prepare for basic facilities and cold nights.

Begin preparing around two to three months beforehand, allowing longer if you are starting from a low fitness level. Gradually combine regular walks, hill hikes and strength exercises. Practise in the boots you will wear on the trek.

Altitude and Acclimatisation

The programme has no dedicated acclimatisation day. Day 3 rises rapidly from Beni to Sidikharka. Discuss the ascent plan before booking; a short itinerary does not remove altitude risk.

Tell your guide immediately about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not continue to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite resting at the same elevation, descent is necessary. Completing the planned walk remains secondary to your health.

Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

If you have an existing medical condition, seek advice from a healthcare professional familiar with high-altitude travel before booking. Discuss your planned route and any medicines you take.

Arrange travel insurance covering the complete agreed trekking route, including approximately 4,190 metres, emergency evacuation and necessary medical treatment. Check altitude limits, activity exclusions, excess charges and assistance procedures. Carry your policy details and an accessible emergency contact number for the whole journey.

Accommodation and Camping Facilities

The route has two Kathmandu nights, one Beni night, four mountain camping nights and one Pokhara night. Confirm hotel categories, room sharing, tent occupancy and included accommodation in your quotation beforehand.

Mountain camps have basic shared facilities, with no reliable promise of hot showers, heated dining rooms or electricity. Tent sites depend on local conditions. Confirm suitable sleeping bags, insulated mats, shelter and toilet arrangements before leaving Kathmandu.

Food and Dietary Requirements

Camp meals are prepared by the trekking kitchen team, with seasonal fruit after dinner. City and road-day meal coverage follows your quotation.

Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before booking so we can discuss what can be arranged along the route. Explain any strict requirements clearly, including concerns about shared cooking equipment.

Drinking Water

Carry reusable bottles and drink boiled or appropriately treated water. Water from a clear stream or an untreated tap may still contain germs.

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs. If boiling is unavailable, filtration followed by disinfection offers broader protection than many portable filters alone. Follow the instructions for your treatment equipment or tablets.

Guide and Porter Support

An English-speaking trekking guide accompanies the mountain route, with porter support for agreed luggage. Confirm crew numbers and luggage allowances before departure. You carry water, weather protection, medicines, documents and daily essentials yourself.

Showers, Charging and Communication

Do not rely on mobile reception, Wi-Fi or device charging above Humkhola. Carry a fully charged power bank and headlamp, and tell family you may be out of contact. Confirm the team's communication arrangements before departure, including how help would be requested if someone becomes unwell.

Permits and Documents

The Annapurna Conservation Area Permit is included. Confirm current trek-registration and document requirements with the company before departure. Carry your passport, insurance details and required copies, and show permits when requested at checkpoints along the route.

Road Delays and Extra Time

Jeep access to Humkhola depends on road conditions. Rain, landslides or road damage can change the pickup point and increase walking or driving time.

Allow extra time before international departure. There is no separate buffer day. Additional accommodation or transport outside the agreed arrangements may cost more money.

The first mountain night is at Sidikharka, reached by jeep and trekking from lower Beni. Overnight stops then rise to Buket Mela and Panch Pokhari, the highest camp. The base camp visit reaches higher ground before descending to Sidikharka. No separate acclimatisation day is included.

Weather and Best Time for the North ABC Camping Trek

Spring, from March to May, and autumn, from September to November, generally suit this northern Annapurna route best. Both seasons offer mountain views, although cold, wind, rain and snow remain possible on the approach and at base camp. Camping requires preparation for low overnight temperatures.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Elevation gradients & route exposure

Beni and the lower approach can feel warm, while nights at Buket Mela and Panch Pokhari are much colder. Wind and cloud can reduce comfort and visibility on higher ground.

Even in spring and autumn, high camps can experience freezing temperatures overnight. Snow or ice may affect the base camp approach, and wet rock can make descents slippery. Carry warm layers, waterproof clothing and protection for your sleeping equipment.

Carry thermal insulation, wind protection, and waterproof gear accessible in your daypack throughout the alpine stages.

Which Season Should You Choose?

Optimal timing for trip priorities

Choose spring for warming days and possible flowers on the lower trail, or autumn for mountain views after the rains. Winter and monsoon departures need careful local assessment of road access, snow, stream crossings and workable camping conditions before booking.

North ABC Camping Trek FAQs

Can I do this trek without previous trekking experience? ∨
Previous mountain hiking and camping experience are recommended. Steep paths, limited facilities and rapid altitude gain make this a challenging short trek. Practise longer hill walks and discuss your experience, health and preparation before choosing the nine-day programme.
What is the highest altitude on this itinerary? ∨
The base camp visit reaches approximately 4,190 metres. The highest overnight camp is near Panch Pokhari in the Panchakunda lake area, around 4,050 metres. Day 6 returns to Sidikharka after the higher visit, subject to conditions.
Is this the usual southern Annapurna Base Camp? ∨
This itinerary approaches Annapurna from Humkhola on the northern side, using camping arrangements near Panchakunda. The southern route reaches a different base camp via Chhomrong and Deurali. Compare the routes, including altitude, facilities and walking demands.
Do we sleep at North Base Camp? ∨
The itinerary camps near Panch Pokhari before the base camp visit. On Day 6, hike towards the viewpoint and return via the lake area to Sidikharka. There is no overnight stay at North Annapurna Base Camp in this programme.
Are there any scheduled acclimatisation days? ∨
No separate acclimatisation day is scheduled. Day 3 gains sleeping altitude from Beni to Sidikharka. Discuss the ascent plan before booking, and do not continue higher if symptoms of altitude illness develop during the trek.
Is insurance covering trekking to 4,000 metres sufficient? ∨
A 4,000-metre limit falls below the planned high camp and base camp visit. Ask your insurer to cover the full agreed route to approximately 4,190 metres, emergency evacuation and medical treatment, including relevant activity exclusions and assistance procedures.
Does being fit protect me from altitude sickness? ∨
Fitness helps with walking but does not prevent altitude illness. This route gains height quickly, particularly between Beni and Sidikharka. Fit trekkers can become unwell. Report symptoms immediately and review the ascent plan before continuing to higher camps.
What should I do if I feel unwell at altitude? ∨
Tell your guide immediately about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not ascend to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite rest at the same elevation, descent is necessary.
Do I need to carry my main trekking bag? ∨
Porter support is included for agreed luggage. Confirm crew numbers, load allowances and camp equipment arrangements before departure. You carry water, weather protection, medicines and daily essentials, keeping your passport, money and valuables with you.
Do I need to bring a sleeping bag and down jacket? ∨
Camping needs a suitable sleeping bag, insulated mat and warm clothing. Confirm supplied equipment and personal hire charges before departure. Check temperature ratings and return conditions, and bring your own boots, toiletries, electronics and other personal essentials.
Can I book as a solo traveller? ∨
Solo travellers can enquire about group departures or a private trek. The listed USD 655 rate applies only to groups of five to seven. Other group sizes need a confirmed quotation before booking the trip.
What does the quoted USD 655 group price include? ∨
The rate is USD 655 per person for five to seven travellers. Guide, porter support, ACAP and mountain camping are identified features. Confirm transport, city accommodation, meals, equipment, staffing and exclusions in an itemised quotation before paying for your booking.
