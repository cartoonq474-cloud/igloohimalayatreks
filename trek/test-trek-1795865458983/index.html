Seasonal Weather Conditions

Spring March–May

Warmer daytime temperatures, blooming rhododendron forests, and pleasant visibility on clear mornings.

Autumn September–November

Crisp post-monsoon air, reliable mountain views, and comfortable walking conditions across high passes.

Winter December–February

Peaceful lodges and bright sunny days with cold mornings and nights at higher elevations.

Monsoon June–August

Warm lush valleys and seasonal rain at lower elevations; ideal for rainshadow Tibetan-border corridors.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Daytime stages feel pleasant in sunshine at lower stops, becoming progressively cooler as altitude increases, especially before sunrise and after sunset. Wind-exposed passes require warm layering.

Which Season Should You Choose?

Choose spring (March–May) for warmer trails and wildflowers, or autumn (October–November) for the crispest panoramic skies. Winter and summer departures suit flexible travellers.
