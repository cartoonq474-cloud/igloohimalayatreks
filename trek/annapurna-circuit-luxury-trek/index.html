Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek — 13 Days

Follow mountain village trails across Thorong La on our 13-day luxury trek through Nepal’s Annapurna region. You’ll walk through Chame, Upper Pisang and Ngawal before reaching Manang, then cross Thorong La to Muktinath and travel by private road transfer to Pokhara. Our itinerary includes a dedicated acclimatisation day in Manang, upgraded stays and a return flight from Pokhara. Alongside the mountain views and village life, expect daily uphill and downhill walking, with demanding sections at high altitude.

Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek Overview

Our 13-day Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek takes you from the forests and villages around Dharapani to the open mountain trails leading towards Thorong La Pass. The journey follows the higher route through Upper Pisang and Ngawal, includes an acclimatisation day in Manang and crosses the 5,416 m pass before descending to the village of Muktinath.

After driving to Dharapani, you follow the Marsyangdi valley through Chame, walking past forests and villages before reaching Upper Pisang. Beyond Pisang, the trail climbs through Ghyaru and continues towards Ngawal, with views across the valley. These villages retain Buddhist monasteries, prayer wheels and traditional houses along the route. You then pass Braga before reaching Manang, where you spend two nights before continuing towards the higher overnight stops.

As you gain altitude, forested paths give way to open slopes and increasingly rocky ground. You continue through Yak Kharka to Thorong Phedi, your overnight base before the long Thorong La crossing. The following day, you cross the pass before descending steeply towards Muktinath. From Muktinath, you drive to Pokhara before flying back to Kathmandu.

We include an acclimatisation day during your Manang stay. This combines a shorter walk with another night at the same elevation before you continue towards Thorong Phedi. You’ll still need to prepare for consecutive walking days, steep climbs and a lengthy descent. Technical climbing skills are not required, but the pass crossing and sustained physical effort make this trek demanding.

You stay in selected trekking lodges and upgraded hotels in Kathmandu and Pokhara. The 13-day programme covers your arrival, the walking journey, return travel and departure from Nepal.

Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek Itinerary — Day by Day

Our 13-day itinerary includes travel from Kathmandu, one acclimatisation day and the Thorong La crossing to Muktinath. Walking times and distances are approximate; your pace, weather and trail conditions can affect each day’s schedule.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu

Arrival & Airport Transfer — Kathmandu (1,400 m) — 5-Star Hotel Welcome

OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m
ACCOMMODATION 5-star hotel in Kathmandu, twin sharing
MEALS Breakfast

Welcome to Nepal. Our representative meets you at Tribhuvan International Airport and transfers you to your hotel. After you settle in, we go through the trekking programme, check your essential equipment and discuss any questions you have.

Depending on your arrival time, you can explore the nearby streets of Thamel or rest after your flight. We also help with any final preparations before you leave for the Annapurna region.

Day 2: Private Drive from Kathmandu to Dharapani

Scenic Mountain Road Transfer — Dharapani (1,860 m) — 8–10 Hrs Drive

DRIVING TIME Approximately 8–10 hours
ROAD DISTANCE Approximately 220–235 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 1,860 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Dharapani
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Your mountain journey begins with a private drive from Kathmandu to Dharapani. The route follows river valleys and winding hill roads, with travel time depending on road conditions and stops.

After breakfast, we leave Kathmandu and travel towards the Annapurna trekking region. The drive passes villages, terraced farmland and forested hillsides, with rougher stretches as you approach the Marsyangdi mountain valley. There are breaks along the way, while the journey introduces you to the landscape around the circuit’s start. We spend the night in Dharapani.

Day 3: Trek from Dharapani to Chame

Pine Forest Trail Ascent — Chame (2,670 m) — 5–6 Hrs Trek

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 15–16 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 2,670 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Chame
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We follow the Marsyangdi valley through villages and pine forests, gaining height steadily along the walking route towards Chame. Your guide sets the pace and allows regular breaks.

The trail includes uphill stretches, with mountain views opening between trees as we continue. Today brings a noticeable gain in sleeping altitude, so we walk steadily rather than rushing towards Chame. The village is the administrative centre of Manang District, with lodges and small shops where you can spend some time after checking into your accommodation.

Day 4: Trek from Chame to Upper Pisang

Paungda Danda Rock Face — Upper Pisang (3,300 m) — 5–6 Hrs Trek

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 13–15 km
STARTING ALTITUDE 2,670 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 3,300 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Upper Pisang
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We continue along the Marsyangdi valley from Chame, through pine forests towards Upper Pisang. The route passes beneath the rock face of Paungda Danda, with views towards the Annapurna range when conditions are clear.

Upper Pisang’s stone houses and prayer flags line village paths above the lower valley. We then settle into accommodation in Upper Pisang. The afternoon gives you time to rest, explore nearby village lanes and prepare for tomorrow’s walk along the higher route.

Day 5: Trek from Upper Pisang to Ngawal

High Route via Ghyaru — Ngawal (3,660 m) — 4–5 Hrs Trek

WALKING TIME Approximately 4–5 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 10–11 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 3,660 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Ngawal
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Leaving Upper Pisang, we follow the higher trail through Ghyaru towards Ngawal. Mountain views accompany sections of the walk, while uphill stretches make this more demanding than following the lower valley.

The trail passes traditional houses and exposed mountain slopes. Once we reach Ngawal, you can settle into the lodge and explore the nearby surroundings. Buddhist monasteries and prayer flags reflect the upper valley’s cultural traditions. If visiting a monastery is possible, your guide explains the local customs before you enter.

Day 6: Trek from Ngawal to Manang

Braga Monastery & Valley Trail — Manang (3,540 m) — 3–4 Hrs Trek

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–4 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 9–10 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 3,540 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Manang
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Ngawal and follow the valley towards Manang, walking beneath the surrounding peaks. Although your next overnight stop is slightly lower, the walk still takes place at altitude, with uneven paths and open slopes.

The route passes Braga’s traditional houses before reaching Manang’s village streets. Today is a shorter walking stage, giving you time to settle in before tomorrow’s planned acclimatisation day. After checking into your lodge, the remaining afternoon is available for rest or a walk nearby.

Day 7: Acclimatisation Day in Manang

Acclimatisation Hike & Rest — Manang (3,540 m) — 3–5 Hrs Excursion

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–5 hours
HIKE DISTANCE Varies with the chosen walk
DAYTIME HIGH POINT Depends on the hike and conditions
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 3,540 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Manang
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Our acclimatisation day includes a shorter walk around Manang followed by another night in the village. The direction and turnaround point depend on your guide’s recommendation, the weather and how everyone is adjusting to altitude.

A local walk gives you a broader view of Manang and its surroundings. We then return to the lodge, leaving the afternoon for rest and preparation. Tomorrow, we continue towards Yak Kharka, where overnight elevations first exceed four thousand metres.

Day 8: Trek from Manang to Yak Kharka

Alpine Pasture Ascent — Yak Kharka (4,050 m) — 4–5 Hrs Trek

WALKING TIME Approximately 4–5 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 9–10 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 4,050 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Yak Kharka
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Manang and continue uphill towards Yak Kharka. After the acclimatisation stop, the route gains height through open slopes above the Manang valley. Yak Kharka lies among mountain pastures where local herders bring their animals to graze.

Beyond Manang, we keep a steady pace as the altitude increases. Even where the gradient eases, the thinner air can make familiar walking distances feel harder. We settle into the lodge and rest before tomorrow’s walk further towards Thorong Phedi.

Day 9: Trek from Yak Kharka to Thorong Phedi

Base of the High Pass — Thorong Phedi (4,450 m) — 4–5 Hrs Trek

WALKING TIME Approximately 4–5 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 7–9 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 4,450 m
ACCOMMODATION Lodge in Thorong Phedi
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We continue from Yak Kharka towards Thorong Phedi. The trail crosses rocky slopes and climbs gradually, with the altitude making the walking demanding. Although the distance is shorter than earlier stages, we allow time for breaks before reaching tonight’s lodge.

After reaching Thorong Phedi, we settle into the lodge and prepare for tomorrow’s crossing. Your guide discusses the early start, checks how you are feeling and reviews your equipment. This is your highest overnight stop, so the remaining afternoon is for rest and preparation.

Day 10: Cross Thorong La Pass and Descend to Muktinath

Thorong La Pass (5,416 m) — Muktinath (3,800 m) — 8–9 Hrs Epic Crossing

WALKING TIME Approximately 8–9 hours
TOTAL TREK DISTANCE Approximately 15–16 km
HIGHEST POINT Thorong La Pass, 5,416 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Muktinath, approximately 3,800 m
ACCOMMODATION Hotel or lodge in Muktinath
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We start before dawn and climb the trail towards Thorong La Pass. The ascent passes rocky slopes and exposed ground, with cold air and altitude making the climb demanding.

Reaching Thorong La at 5,416 m brings you to the itinerary’s highest point. Prayer flags mark the pass, with mountain views possible when conditions allow. The next stage is a long descent on the Mustang side.

After a short stop at the pass, we descend the trail towards Muktinath. The lower overnight stop gives you time to rest after the longest walking day.

Day 11: Private Drive from Muktinath to Pokhara

Kali Gandaki Gorge Private Drive — Pokhara (822 m) — 5-Star Lakeside Hotel

MAIN ACTIVITY Private drive from Muktinath to Pokhara
DRIVING TIME Full-day journey; varies with road conditions
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 822 m
ACCOMMODATION 5-star hotel in Pokhara
MEALS Breakfast; other meals as confirmed

We leave Muktinath by private vehicle and drive down towards Pokhara. The road follows the Kali Gandaki valley, bringing a gradual change from dry mountain slopes to greener hillsides.

The journey passes Jomsom and continues through the valley, with stops along the way as needed. Driving time depends on road conditions and stops. Although trekking is complete, plan for a full-day road journey before arriving in Pokhara. After checking into your hotel, you have time to rest after travelling.

Day 12: Fly from Pokhara to Kathmandu

Scenic Himalayan Flight — Kathmandu (1,400 m) — Farewell Dinner

MAIN ACTIVITY Flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu, taking approximately 25–30 minutes
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m
ACCOMMODATION 5-star hotel in Kathmandu
MEALS Breakfast and farewell dinner

We prepare for the return flight from Pokhara. The short domestic journey takes you back to Kathmandu, with departure time depending on the weather and airline operating schedule.

After reaching your hotel, the remaining time is free for rest, shopping or exploring nearby areas. We share a farewell dinner in Kathmandu, bringing the trekking programme to a close. We arrange your transfers between the airports and hotels.

Day 13: Departure from Kathmandu

Final Departure — Tribhuvan International Airport — Journey End

MAIN ACTIVITY Departure from Nepal
MEALS Breakfast

Your 13-day Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek ends today. After breakfast, you prepare for your onward journey. Depending on your departure time, there may be an opportunity for a final walk nearby before leaving the hotel.

Our representative transfers you to Tribhuvan International Airport for your departure.

Trek Difficulty and Fitness

The Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek is physically demanding, although it does not require technical climbing skills. You will walk over steep paths, stone steps and uneven ground on consecutive days. Most stages take three to six hours; crossing Thorong La usually takes eight to nine hours.

Previous hiking experience is helpful. First-time trekkers should build their endurance before departure and become comfortable walking uphill and downhill with a daypack.

Begin preparing around two to three months beforehand, allowing longer if you are starting from a low fitness level. Gradually combine regular walks, hill hikes and strength exercises. Practise in the boots you will wear on the trek.

Altitude and Acclimatisation

Our itinerary includes one dedicated acclimatisation day before leaving Manang. This gives you time to adjust before continuing higher, but altitude illness can still affect you, regardless of fitness.

Tell your guide promptly about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not continue to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite resting at the same elevation, descent is necessary. Crossing Thorong La must remain secondary to your health.

Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

If you have an existing medical condition, seek advice from a healthcare professional familiar with high-altitude travel before booking. Discuss your planned route and any medicines you take.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended before departure. Confirm that your policy covers trekking to at least 5,416 metres, helicopter evacuation and medical treatment in Nepal. Check exclusions, excess charges and the insurer’s emergency assistance procedure. Carry your policy details and emergency contact number.

Accommodation and Room Facilities

You will stay in five-star hotels in Kathmandu and Pokhara, with selected lodges while trekking. City hotels use twin-sharing rooms; private or single rooms cost extra and are subject to availability.

Higher on the route, expect simpler rooms and shared bathroom facilities. Comfort varies between lodges, and private rooms can be limited during busy periods. Prepare for cold nights and fewer facilities around Yak Kharka and Thorong Phedi.

Food and Dietary Requirements

Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included during the trek. Teahouse menus commonly offer dal bhat, noodles, potatoes, soups and other straightforward meals.

Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before booking so we can discuss what can be arranged along the route. Explain any strict requirements clearly, including concerns about shared cooking equipment.

Drinking Water

Carry reusable bottles and drink boiled or appropriately treated water. Water from a clear stream or an untreated tap may still contain germs.

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs. If boiling is unavailable, filtration followed by disinfection offers broader protection than many portable filters alone. Follow the instructions for your treatment equipment or tablets.

Guide and Porter Support

We provide a licensed, English-speaking guide, with one porter normally shared between two trekkers. Your porter carries the main trekking luggage; you carry your daypack and the items you need during the walk.

Showers, Charging and Communication

Lodge services, including hot showers, Wi-Fi and charging, may cost extra where available. Mobile reception and internet connections can be unreliable, particularly higher on the route. Carry a power bank and let family know that you may be out of contact for periods of the trek.

Permits and Documents

We arrange the Annapurna Conservation Area Permit, TIMS card and any applicable local trekking fees as part of your package. Carry your passport, insurance details and copies of important documents. Your Nepal visa remains your responsibility.

Flight Delays and Extra Time

Pokhara flights can be delayed or cancelled because of weather. Road conditions can affect private transfers, so travel times may vary throughout this itinerary.

Allow extra time before your international departure. Additional accommodation and alternative transport may cost extra. Consider an extra Kathmandu night when planning onward flights.

Weather and Best Time for the Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek

Spring, from March to May, and autumn, from September to November, are generally the best seasons for the Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek. Both offer favourable walking conditions and opportunities for mountain views, although cold weather, cloud and snowfall remain possible higher on the route.

Seasonal Weather Conditions

4 Seasons Matrix
Season Months What to Expect
Spring March–May Days gradually become warmer. March can still feel cold around Manang and Thorong Phedi, while April usually offers a good balance of walking temperatures and visibility. May is warmer, with possible afternoon haze or cloud.
Autumn September–November Early September can retain monsoon rain. October and November generally offer clearer mountain views after the rains, with colder mornings and nights as winter approaches.
Winter December–February Clear days and quieter trails are possible, but cold, shorter daylight hours and snow or ice make the higher sections more demanding. Snowfall can prevent crossing Thorong La.
Monsoon June–August, sometimes extending into September Rain affects lower trails and roads; Manang and Mustang are drier. Paths may be wet and slippery, and poor visibility can disrupt Pokhara flights.

These are seasonal patterns; conditions can vary from year to year and during an individual trek.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Elevation & Microclimates

Dharapani and Chame can feel relatively warm when you walk in sunshine. Conditions become colder as you approach Manang, Yak Kharka and Thorong Phedi, particularly before sunrise and after sunset.

Even during spring and autumn, nights on the higher route can fall below freezing. Exposed sections, including the climb to Thorong La, can also feel colder in the wind. Carry warm layers and weather protection whichever month you choose.

Which Season Should You Choose?

Trekker Timing Advice

Choose spring if you prefer gradually warming days and longer daylight for your daily walks. Choose autumn, particularly October or November, if mountain visibility is your main priority. Winter and monsoon require flexibility because snow or rain can disrupt travel.

Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek FAQs

Clear answers to the most common questions about the Annapurna Circuit Luxury Trek, Thorong La Pass crossing, accommodation, and preparation.

General & Route

Can I do this trek without previous trekking experience? ∨
Yes, provided you prepare well and have suitable fitness. Previous hiking experience helps, but technical climbing skills are unnecessary. Before departure, practise longer hill walks with a daypack and become comfortable walking uphill and downhill on consecutive days.
What is the highest altitude on this itinerary? ∨
The highest planned point is Thorong La Pass at 5,416 metres, which you cross before descending to Muktinath. Your highest overnight stop is Thorong Phedi at approximately 4,450 metres, where you stay before the pass crossing.
Do we stay overnight at Thorong La? ∨
No. You cross Thorong La during the day and then descend to a lodge in Muktinath for the night. The previous night is spent at Thorong Phedi before an early morning departure. Camping at the pass is not included.
How does the 14-day package differ from this itinerary? ∨
Our Annapurna Circuit Trek — 14 Days includes a walk from Muktinath to Jomsom via Kagbeni before travelling by road to Pokhara. This 13-day luxury itinerary uses private transport from Muktinath, upgraded accommodation and a flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu.
Is acclimatisation just a rest day? ∨
It usually includes a shorter hike above Manang towards nearby viewpoints, followed by a return to the same lodge to sleep. Your guide adjusts the hike according to conditions and how the group is feeling.
Is insurance covering trekking to 5,500 metres sufficient? ∨
A 5,500-metre limit exceeds Thorong La’s 5,416 metres, but other policy terms determine the actual coverage. Ask your insurer to confirm coverage for the full route, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment, including any relevant exclusions or conditions.
Does being fit protect me from altitude sickness? ∨
No. Good fitness helps with the walking demands, but it does not prevent altitude illness. Fit and experienced trekkers can also become unwell. Follow the acclimatisation schedule and report symptoms promptly instead of assuming fitness will protect you.
What should I do if I feel unwell at altitude? ∨
Tell your guide promptly about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not ascend to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite rest at the same elevation, descent is necessary.
Does luxury mean five-star accommodation throughout the trek? ∨
Five-star hotels are included in Kathmandu and Pokhara, with breakfast each morning. During the trek, we select comfortable lodges from the available local options. Facilities become simpler at higher elevations, where shared bathrooms may be necessary.
Do I need to carry my main trekking bag? ∨
A porter carries your main luggage, with one porter normally shared between two trekkers. You carry your daypack containing water, weather protection, medicines and other daily essentials. Keep your passport, money and valuables with you.
Do I need to bring a sleeping bag and down jacket? ∨
We provide a sleeping bag and down jacket for your use during the trek at no charge. A duffel bag is also supplied. You remain responsible for personal items such as trekking boots, clothing, toiletries and electronics.
Can I book as a solo traveller? ∨
Yes. You can arrange a private trek, with solo prices starting from USD 2,799. Send us your preferred dates so we can check the available options and confirm the price that applies to your booking.
