Everest Cho La and Renjo La Pass Trek — 19 Days

Reach Everest Base Camp, hike Kala Patthar and cross Cho La and Renjo La on this 19-day circuit through the Khumbu. The route follows Namche Bazaar, Tengboche and Dingboche before turning towards Dzongla and the Gokyo Lakes. Spend two nights in Gokyo, including a Gokyo Ri hike, then descend through Lungden and Thame. Acclimatisation days in Namche and Dingboche form part of a demanding itinerary with high passes, glacier terrain and long walking days above the valleys.

Everest Two Passes Trek Overview

Our 19-day Everest Two Passes Trek combines Everest Base Camp, Kala Patthar and Gokyo Lakes with crossings of Cho La and Renjo La. You follow the main Khumbu trail through Namche and Dingboche before turning towards Gokyo. After hiking Gokyo Ri, cross into the western valley and return through Lungden and Thame towards Namche Bazaar.

After flying to Lukla, walk through Phakding and cross suspension bridges before climbing to Namche Bazaar. We spend two nights here, including an acclimatisation hike above the town. Continue through Tengboche, where a monastery stands beneath Ama Dablam, and onwards to Dingboche. Village homes, mani walls and prayer wheels reflect the Buddhist traditions of the Khumbu, while forest paths gradually give way to open slopes and rocky ground.

Beyond Dingboche, continue through Lobuche to Gorak Shep for the Everest Base Camp visit. Hike Kala Patthar the following morning, then follow the trail towards Dzongla. Cho La links this side of the Khumbu with Thangnak and Gokyo. After Gokyo Ri, cross Renjo La and descend through Lungden and Thame, completing the circuit through Namche.

Acclimatisation days in Namche and Dingboche give you additional nights at the same elevation before continuing higher. This remains a very demanding route, with consecutive walking days, glacier terrain and two passes above 5,000 metres. Previous mountain trekking experience is strongly recommended. Snow, ice and changing weather affect both crossings, so your guide assesses conditions and daily progress before continuing.

You stay in mountain teahouses during the trek and a hotel in Kathmandu. The 19-day programme covers your arrival, the walking circuit, return travel and departure from Nepal.

Everest Two Passes Trek Highlights

Reach Everest Base Camp at 5,364 m, with the Khumbu Glacier and surrounding peaks marking the first major objective of your circuit.
Hike Kala Patthar for close views of Everest, Nuptse and Pumori when the weather is clear.
Cross Cho La, then continue over the Ngozumpa Glacier to Gokyo Village beside the lakes and mountains.
Hike Gokyo Ri for Everest, Lhotse, Makalu and Cho Oyu views in clear weather.
Cross Renjo La and descend through Lungden and Thame, following a different valley back towards Namche.
Spend acclimatisation days in Namche and Dingboche, combining shorter hikes with additional nights at the same elevation before continuing higher.

Everest Two Passes Trek Itinerary — Day by Day

Our 19-day itinerary includes Everest Base Camp, Kala Patthar, Gokyo Ri and two demanding high passes. Walking times and distances are approximate; your pace, weather, trail conditions and health can affect each day’s schedule.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu

Arrival & Airport Transfer — Kathmandu, approximately 1,400 m — 3-Star Hotel Check-in

OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m
ACCOMMODATION 3-star hotel in Kathmandu
MEALS Breakfast; lunch and dinner at own expense

Welcome to Nepal. Our representative meets you at Tribhuvan International Airport and accompanies you to your hotel. After you settle in, we go through the trekking programme, check your essential equipment and discuss any questions you have.

Depending on your arrival time, you can explore the nearby streets of Thamel or rest after your flight. We also help with any final preparations before you leave for the Everest region.

Day 2: Fly to Lukla and Trek to Phakding

Morning Mountain Flight & Valley Trek — Phakding, 2,610 m — 3–4 hrs walk (7–8 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–4 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 7–8 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 2,610 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Phakding
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Your mountain journey begins with a flight to Lukla at 2,860 m. Depending on airline operations and the season, the flight may depart from Kathmandu or Manthali Airport in Ramechhap.

After landing, you meet your trekking team and begin walking towards Phakding. The trail passes village homes, prayer wheels and forested slopes, with downhill stretches and shorter climbs along the way. Sections follow the Dudh Koshi River, introducing you to the valley you will explore over the coming days. We spend the night in Phakding.

Day 3: Trek from Phakding to Namche Bazaar

Sagarmatha National Park & Hillary Bridges — Namche Bazaar, 3,440 m — 5–6 hrs walk (10–11 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 10–11 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 3,440 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Namche Bazaar
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We follow the Dudh Koshi through villages and pine forests, passing Benkar and Monjo before entering Sagarmatha National Park. Your guide handles the permit checks along the route.

Suspension bridges connect the valley trails, followed by a sustained uphill walk towards Namche. This final climb is the most demanding part of the day, so we allow time for breaks. Namche’s shops, lodges and houses spread across the hillside, making it an important trading town and a useful place to spend the next two nights.

Day 4: Acclimatisation Day in Namche Bazaar

Acclimatisation Hike to Hotel Everest View — Namche Bazaar, 3,440 m (Daytime High 3,880 m) — 3–4 hrs (4–5 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–4 hours
HIKE DISTANCE Approximately 4–5 km
DAYTIME HIGH POINT Around 3,880 m if visiting Hotel Everest View
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 3,440 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Namche Bazaar
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We stay another night in Namche and take a shorter hike above the town. A usual option is the trail towards Hotel Everest View, where clear weather offers views of Everest and nearby peaks.

Your guide chooses the walk according to conditions and how the group is feeling. Afterwards, we return to Namche for the night. The afternoon gives you time to rest, visit the shops or stop at a café before continuing towards Tengboche tomorrow.

Day 5: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Tengboche

Dudh Koshi Valley & Tengboche Monastery — Tengboche, around 3,860 m — 5–6 hrs walk (9–10 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 9–10 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 3,860 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Tengboche
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Leaving Namche, we follow the hillside trail before descending towards Phunki Tenga. Mountain views accompany sections of the walk, while the river marks the low point before the climb to Tengboche.

The uphill trail passes through pine and rhododendron forest. Once we reach the village, you can settle into the teahouse and explore the surroundings. Tengboche’s Buddhist monastery stands against the mountain backdrop of Ama Dablam. If it is open to visitors, your guide explains the visiting customs before you enter.

Day 6: Trek from Tengboche to Dingboche

Debuche, Pangboche & Imja Valley — Dingboche, 4,410 m — 5–6 hrs walk (10–12 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 10–12 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 4,410 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Dingboche
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We descend through forest towards Debuche, cross the Imja Khola and continue through Pangboche. As the route gains height, the trees become fewer and the valley opens into slopes with low vegetation and stone buildings.

The trail passes Somare before reaching Dingboche in the Imja Valley. Today brings a noticeable gain in sleeping altitude, so we keep a steady pace throughout the walk. After checking into your teahouse, the remaining afternoon is available for rest before tomorrow’s acclimatisation hike.

Day 7: Acclimatisation Day in Dingboche

Nangkartshang Viewpoint Ridge Hike — Dingboche, 4,410 m — 3–4 hrs (4–5 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–4 hours
HIKE DISTANCE Approximately 4–5 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 4,410 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Dingboche
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Our second acclimatisation day includes a hike above Dingboche followed by another night in the village. The usual direction is towards the Nangkartshang viewpoint, with the turnaround point determined by conditions and the group’s progress.

The hillside gives you a broader view of Dingboche and the surrounding valley. We then return to the teahouse, leaving the afternoon for rest and preparation. Tomorrow, the route continues towards Lobuche, where the terrain becomes increasingly rocky and glacial.

Day 8: Trek from Dingboche to Lobuche

Thukla Pass Memorial Ridge & Khumbu Moraine — Lobuche, around 4,910 m — 5–6 hrs walk (8–9 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 5–6 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 8–9 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Around 4,910 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Lobuche
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Dingboche and cross open ground towards Thukla. After a break, the trail climbs more steeply to the memorial area above the village. The stone memorials commemorate climbers and Sherpas who lost their lives in the mountains.

Beyond this section, we continue beside the Khumbu Glacier towards Lobuche. Uneven ground and increasing altitude make the walking more demanding, even where the gradient eases. We settle into the teahouse and prepare for tomorrow’s journey to Everest Base Camp.

Day 9: Trek from Lobuche to Everest Base Camp and Gorak Shep

Everest Base Camp (5,364 m) Expedition Hike — Gorak Shep, 5,164 m — 7–8 hrs (12–15 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 7–8 hours
TOTAL TREK DISTANCE Approximately 12–15 km
HIGHEST POINT Everest Base Camp, 5,364 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Gorak Shep, approximately 5,164 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Gorak Shep
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We start from Lobuche and follow the rocky trail towards Gorak Shep. After a break, we continue to Everest Base Camp, crossing uneven glacial ground along the Khumbu Glacier.

Reaching base camp brings you into the landscape used by Everest expedition teams. During the spring climbing season, tents and expedition activity may be visible. At other times, the glacier and surrounding mountains define the visit.

After spending time at base camp, we retrace the trail to Gorak Shep. Tonight’s stop puts us close to the starting point for tomorrow’s Kala Patthar hike.

Day 10: Hike to Kala Patthar and Trek to Dzongla

Kala Patthar Summit (5,545 m) & Trek to Pass Base — Dzongla, approximately 4,830 m — 7–8 hrs (12–14 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 7–8 hours
TOTAL TREK DISTANCE Approximately 12–14 km
HIGHEST POINT Kala Patthar viewpoint, approximately 5,545 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Dzongla, approximately 4,830 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Dzongla
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We make an early start for Kala Patthar. The climb above Gorak Shep is steep in places, and the altitude makes it demanding. In clear weather, the viewpoint offers close views of Everest, Nuptse, Pumori and the surrounding Khumbu peaks.

After the hike, return to Gorak Shep for breakfast and walk towards Lobuche. We then leave the main trail for Dzongla, following rocky slopes with views across the surrounding mountains in clear weather. Settle into your teahouse and prepare for tomorrow’s Cho La crossing.

Day 11: Cross Cho La Pass and Trek to Thangnak

PASS 1: Cho La Pass (5,420 m) Glacier Crossing — Thangnak, approximately 4,700 m — 7–9 hrs (9–10 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 7–9 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 9–10 km
HIGHEST POINT Cho La Pass, approximately 5,420 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Thangnak, approximately 4,700 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Thangnak
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Dzongla early and climb towards Cho La. Rocky paths lead to glacier terrain, where snow and ice can affect footing. Your guide assesses conditions before continuing higher.

The pass connects the Everest Base Camp side of the Khumbu with the Gokyo Valley. Follow your guide closely across the upper section, then begin the descent over steep, uneven ground towards the lower valley below.

The descent continues to Thangnak, where we settle into a teahouse. Keep a steady pace throughout this demanding stage and tell your guide if you feel unwell.

Day 12: Trek from Thangnak to Gokyo

Ngozumpa Glacier Crossing to Third Lake — Gokyo, approximately 4,790 m — 3–4 hrs (4–5 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–4 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 4–5 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 4,790 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Gokyo
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Today’s shorter stage crosses the Ngozumpa Glacier towards Gokyo. The route passes over rocky debris and uneven moraine, with small climbs and descents along the way. Follow your guide’s chosen path across the changing terrain.

After leaving the glacier, continue towards Gokyo Village beside Dudh Pokhari, the third lake. We spend two nights here. Settle into your teahouse, rest and organise your warm layers for tomorrow’s viewpoint hike. Explore the nearby lakeside paths if your energy and conditions allow.

Day 13: Hike to Gokyo Ri and Return to Gokyo

Sunrise Climb to Gokyo Ri (5,357 m) Viewpoint — Gokyo, approximately 4,790 m — 3–5 hrs (4–6 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 3–5 hours for the return hike
HIKE DISTANCE Approximately 4–6 km
DAYTIME HIGH POINT Gokyo Ri, approximately 5,357 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 4,790 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Gokyo
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We start early for the climb to Gokyo Ri. The path rises above the village, with the altitude adding effort to the steep ascent. Walk steadily and keep warm layers accessible before the sun rises.

In clear weather, the viewpoint offers views of Everest, Lhotse, Makalu and Cho Oyu, with Gokyo Lakes and the Ngozumpa Glacier below. Return carefully to Gokyo and rest. Your guide reviews conditions and your progress before tomorrow’s Renjo La crossing.

Day 14: Cross Renjo La Pass and Trek to Lungden

PASS 2: Renjo La Pass (5,360 m) & Western Valley Descent — Lungden, approximately 4,380 m — 7–8 hrs (12–14 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 7–8 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 12–14 km
HIGHEST POINT Renjo La Pass, approximately 5,360 m
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Lungden, approximately 4,380 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Lungden
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

We leave Gokyo early and climb towards Renjo La. The ascent is steep, with rocky ground and possible snow or ice. Your guide assesses conditions and your progress throughout.

From the pass, clear weather gives views across Gokyo Lakes towards Everest and the surrounding peaks. After a break, begin the long descent over stone steps and uneven paths into the western valley of the Khumbu.

Continue towards Lungden in the Bhote Koshi valley, where we stay in a teahouse. Both passes are behind you, although the remaining trek still includes uphill stretches.

Day 15: Trek from Lungden to Thame

Bhote Koshi Valley & Historic Sherpa Village — Thame, approximately 3,800 m — 4–5 hrs (9–10 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 4–5 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 9–10 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 3,800 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Thame
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

From Lungden, follow the Bhote Koshi valley towards Thame. The trail loses height through grazing country, passing stone-walled fields, village homes and Buddhist features. After yesterday’s long crossing, today allows a steadier walk through the lower part of the valley.

Thame is a Sherpa village known for its monastery and links with Himalayan mountaineering. Settle into your teahouse and rest. If time and conditions allow, ask your guide about exploring nearby paths or visiting the monastery, following local customs and any current access arrangements.

Day 16: Trek from Thame to Namche Bazaar

Circuit Completion to Sherpa Capital — Namche Bazaar, 3,440 m — 4–5 hrs (10–11 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 4–5 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 10–11 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 3,440 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Namche Bazaar
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Leaving Thame, follow the valley back towards Namche Bazaar. The route passes village homes and Buddhist features before returning to wooded slopes, with uphill stretches between the descending sections.

As we approach Namche, familiar shops, lodges and cafés come into view. Check into your teahouse after completing the circuit through Gokyo and the western valley. The rest of the day allows time to organise your belongings, visit nearby shops or rest before tomorrow’s final trekking stage back to Lukla.

Day 17: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Lukla

Dudh Koshi Valley Descent & Final Trek Day — Lukla, 2,860 m — 6–8 hrs (17–19 km)

WALKING TIME Approximately 6–8 hours
TREK DISTANCE Approximately 17–19 km
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE 2,860 m
ACCOMMODATION Teahouse in Lukla
MEALS Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Our final trekking day follows the route down from Namche through Jorsalle, Monjo and Phakding. You cross familiar suspension bridges and return through the forested sections of the Dudh Koshi valley.

The approach to Lukla includes uphill stretches, so there is still some effort ahead after the main descent. On arrival, we check into the teahouse for the last night in the mountains. The evening gives you time to organise your belongings and spend time with your guide and porter team.

Day 18: Fly from Lukla and Return to Kathmandu

Scenic Flight to Kathmandu & Farewell Dinner — Kathmandu, approximately 1,400 m — 3-Star Hotel

MAIN ACTIVITY Flight from Lukla and onward travel to Kathmandu
OVERNIGHT ALTITUDE Approximately 1,400 m
ACCOMMODATION 3-star hotel in Kathmandu
MEALS Breakfast and farewell dinner

We prepare for the return flight from Lukla. Depending on the operating schedule, you may fly directly to Kathmandu or land at Manthali Airport and continue by road.

After reaching your hotel, the remaining time is free for rest, shopping or exploring nearby areas. We share a farewell dinner in Kathmandu, bringing the trekking programme to a close. Flight timing remains dependent on weather and airline operations.

Day 19: Departure from Kathmandu

Final Souvenir Shopping & International Airport Transfer — Home Sweet Home

MAIN ACTIVITY Departure from Nepal
MEALS Breakfast

Your 19-day Everest Two Passes Trek ends today. After breakfast, you prepare for your onward journey. Depending on your departure time, there may be an opportunity for a final walk nearby before leaving the hotel.

Our representative accompanies you to Tribhuvan International Airport for your departure.

1. Trek Difficulty and Fitness

Very challenging high-pass circuit requiring steep climbs, glacier travel, and cardiovascular endurance.

The Everest Two Passes Trek involves steep paths, glacier terrain and repeated days at high altitude. Cho La and Renjo La require sustained climbs and long descents. Snow and ice can increase the difficulty. Prepare for consecutive walking days and discuss your previous mountain trekking experience.

Previous high-altitude trekking experience is strongly recommended. Build endurance through longer hill walks with a daypack, and become comfortable on steep, uneven paths.

Begin preparing around two to three months beforehand, allowing longer if you are starting from a low fitness level. Gradually combine regular walks, hill hikes and strength exercises. Practise in the boots you will wear on the trek.

2. Altitude and Acclimatisation

Acclimatisation rest days in Namche & Dingboche with daily health monitoring.

Our itinerary includes acclimatisation days in Namche and Dingboche before the higher stages. Additional nights help, but altitude illness remains possible. Your guide reviews conditions and how you feel.

Tell your guide immediately about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not continue to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite resting at the same elevation, descent is necessary. Completing either pass must remain secondary to your health.

3. Medical Conditions and Travel Insurance

Mandatory emergency helicopter evacuation & high-altitude medical policy up to 5,545 m.

If you have an existing medical condition, seek advice from a healthcare professional familiar with high-altitude travel before booking. Discuss your planned route and any medicines you take.

Travel insurance is compulsory for this package. Confirm cover for the route, including Kala Patthar at approximately 5,545 metres, both passes, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment. Check altitude restrictions, exclusions, excess charges and emergency procedures. Carry your policy details and emergency contacts.

4. Accommodation and Room Facilities

3-star hotel in Kathmandu & twin-share teahouses with private facilities at key stops.

You stay in a three-star Kathmandu hotel and twin-sharing teahouses along the route. We include two hotel nights and sixteen teahouse nights, with Gorak Shep the highest overnight stop en route.

Higher lodges have simpler rooms with shared bathrooms. Private bathrooms and hot showers are included in Phakding, Namche and Lukla. Confirm room arrangements and upgrades before departure. Prepare for cold nights around Gorak Shep, Dzongla and Gokyo.

5. Food and Dietary Requirements

Three nutritious meals daily on trek, hot drinks, seasonal fruit and dinner Snickers.

Trekking meals include breakfast, lunch and dinner, with tea or coffee at each meal, plus seasonal fruit and Snickers chocolate with dinner.

Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before booking so we can discuss what can be arranged along the route. Explain any strict requirements clearly, including concerns about shared cooking equipment.

6. Drinking Water

Boiled and treated water precautions for safe hydration along trail.

Carry reusable bottles and drink boiled or appropriately treated water. Water from a clear stream or an untreated tap may still contain germs.

Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs. If boiling is unavailable, filtration followed by disinfection offers broader protection than many portable filters alone. Follow the instructions for your treatment equipment or tablets.

7. Guide and Porter Support

Experienced licensed English-speaking guides and 1 porter between two trekkers.

An experienced, English-speaking guide and one porter per two trekkers support your route. Porter meals, accommodation, insurance and wages are included. You carry your daypack; confirm porter loads and luggage allowances before departure.

8. Showers, Charging and Communication

Included hot showers at Phakding, Namche & Lukla; higher alpine connectivity advice.

Private bathrooms and hot showers are included at Phakding, Namche and Lukla. Other showers, Wi-Fi, charging, laundry and phone calls cost extra. Facilities vary, and mobile reception can be unreliable. Carry a power bank and tell family you may be out of contact during the trek.

9. Permits and Documents

Sagarmatha National Park entry & Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality fees included.

We arrange the Sagarmatha National Park permit and Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality entry permit as part of the package. Carry your passport, insurance details and copies of important documents. Your Nepal visa remains your responsibility.

Mountain weather contingency planning for Lukla and Manthali Airport flights.

Lukla flights can be delayed or cancelled because of weather. During peak periods, flights may use Manthali Airport in Ramechhap, requiring a road transfer.

Weather and Best Time for the Everest Two Passes Trek

Spring, from March to May, and autumn, from September to November, are the main planning seasons for the Everest Two Passes Trek. Both offer opportunities for mountain views, although cold weather, cloud and snowfall remain possible around the higher villages and on both passes.

Seasonal Weather Conditions

Season Months What to Expect
Spring March–May Lower trails gradually become warmer, with rhododendrons around Namche and Tengboche. March can retain winter snow near both passes. April and May offer longer daylight, although afternoon cloud and changing high-altitude conditions remain possible.
Autumn September–November Early September can retain monsoon cloud and rain. October and November often offer clearer mountain mornings, with colder nights and possible snow around the higher villages, Cho La and Renjo La.
Winter December–February Severe cold, short daylight and snow or ice can make either pass inaccessible. Confirm current access, lodge availability and the suitability of any planned crossing with your guide before continuing.
Monsoon June–August, sometimes extending into September Rain and cloud can slow walking, limit mountain views and disrupt Lukla flights. Glacier and pass conditions require local assessment, with flexibility for changes rather than assuming the full circuit remains accessible.

These are seasonal patterns; conditions can vary from year to year and during an individual trek.

How Weather Changes Along the Route

Lower Valleys & Settlements

Walking around Phakding and Namche can feel warm in sunshine. Conditions become colder around Dingboche, Lobuche, Gorak Shep and Gokyo, particularly before sunrise, after sunset and on the exposed passes.

High Passes & Ridge Viewpoints

During spring and autumn, nights on the higher route can fall below freezing. Wind makes the viewpoints and passes feel colder, while snow or ice affects footing. Carry warm layers, gloves and weather protection, and follow your guide’s advice.

Which Season Should You Choose?

Spring: Rhododendrons & Warming Days

Choose spring if you prefer gradually warming days and flowering forests on the lower trail.

Autumn: Crystal Mountain Visibility

Choose autumn, particularly October or November, if mountain visibility is your main priority.

Winter & Monsoon Flexibility

Winter and monsoon require greater flexibility and preparation for less comfortable conditions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everest Two Passes Trek FAQs

Essential answers about high passes, required fitness, teahouse facilities, and trek logistics for the 19-day journey.

General & Route

Can I do this trek without previous trekking experience?

Previous mountain trekking experience is strongly recommended. This route includes glacier terrain, long walking days and two high passes. Prepare through hill walks with a daypack, and discuss your experience, fitness and ability on uneven ground with us.

What is the highest altitude on this itinerary?

The highest planned point is Kala Patthar at approximately 5,545 metres, above Everest Base Camp at 5,364 metres. Your highest overnight stop is Gorak Shep at approximately 5,164 metres, where you stay after visiting Base Camp.

Can I see Mount Everest from Base Camp?

Everest’s summit is generally hidden behind nearby ridges from Base Camp itself. Kala Patthar provides the clearer view of Everest that many travellers expect. Our itinerary includes this viewpoint, although mountain visibility depends on the weather.

Do we sleep at Everest Base Camp?

No. You visit Base Camp during the day and return to a teahouse in Gorak Shep for the night. The following morning includes the planned Kala Patthar hike before continuing towards Dzongla. Camping at Base Camp is not included.

Does this itinerary cross all three Everest high passes?

No. This route crosses Cho La and Renjo La, with Everest Base Camp and Gokyo included. Kongma La belongs to the Everest Three High Passes Trek, which adds stages through Chhukung before joining the trail towards Lobuche and Base Camp.

Do I need to carry my main trekking bag?

A porter carries your main trekking luggage, with one porter shared between two trekkers. You carry your daypack containing water, weather protection, medicines and other daily essentials. Keep your passport, money and valuables with you.

Do I need to bring a sleeping bag and down jacket?

We provide a sleeping bag, down jacket and duffel bag for use during the trek. Return the loan equipment afterwards. You prepare personal items such as trekking boots, clothing, toiletries, electronics and anything needed for your health.

Are acclimatisation days complete rest days?

They usually include a shorter hike above Namche Bazaar or Dingboche, followed by a return to the same village to sleep. Your guide adjusts the hike according to conditions and how the group is feeling.

Does being fit protect me from altitude sickness?

No. Good fitness helps with the walking demands, but it does not prevent altitude illness. Fit and experienced trekkers can also become unwell. Follow the acclimatisation schedule and report symptoms immediately instead of assuming fitness will protect you.

What should I do if I feel unwell at altitude?

Tell your guide immediately about headaches, nausea, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Do not ascend to a higher sleeping altitude while experiencing symptoms of altitude illness. If symptoms worsen despite rest at the same elevation, descent is necessary.

Can I book as a solo traveller?

Yes. Published solo rates are available. Send us your preferred dates so we can check group or private arrangements and confirm a quotation for the full 19-day itinerary, accommodation and porter support that you need.

Is insurance covering trekking to 5,500 metres sufficient?

A limit of 5,500 metres falls below the planned Kala Patthar elevation of approximately 5,545 metres. Ask your insurer to confirm coverage for the full route, emergency helicopter evacuation and medical treatment, including any relevant exclusions or conditions.
