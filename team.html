Unsung Mountain Heroes

Our Field Support Crew & Porters

Porters and assistant guides are the backbone of Himalayan trekking. They carry heavy loads across 5,000m passes, set up camps, and ensure gear arrives safely at teahouses before you get there.

Our 100% Porter Welfare Commitment:
  • Strict 20kg maximum weight load per porter
  • Above-industry daily wages & prompt payment
  • High-altitude jackets, boots & gloves provided
  • Emergency medical & helicopter rescue insurance
  • Guaranteed warm teahouse food & shelter
