Why Trek in Ganesh Himal?

  • Striking views of Ganesh Himal I (7,422m), II, III, and IV
  • Rich mineral deposits including natural rubies and zinc mines
  • Community homestays supporting local Tamang and Gurung families
  • Pangsang Pass (3,842m) offering sweeping Himalayan views
  • Relaxing natural hot springs at Tatopani
  • Pristine, quiet trails close to Kathmandu valley

Altitude & Safety

Max Altitude: 3,842m (Pangsang Pass).

Daily pulse oximeter monitoring by Sherpa guides and planned acclimatization days.
