Our Workplace Ethics

Why Work With Igloo Himalaya Treks?

We believe that extraordinary guest experiences start with a valued, well-supported team on the trail and in the office.

Fair Pay & Porter Welfare

We enforce strict load limits (20kg max), provide full high-altitude insurance, supply warm technical gear, and pay well above industry standards.

Safety First & Insurance

Every field guide and staff member is covered under comprehensive emergency medical and helicopter evacuation insurance on all expeditions.

Training & Certification

We sponsor NMA/TAAN mountaineering courses, Wilderness First Responder (WFR) training, and language enrichment programs for our team.

Eco-Trekking & Conservation

Join an organization committed to Leave No Trace principles, trail cleanup projects, and economic empowerment of Himalayan mountain villages.
