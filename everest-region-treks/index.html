Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best treks in the Everest region?
The top treks in the Khumbu region include the classic Everest Base Camp Trek (14 Days), Gokyo Lakes Trek (14 Days), Three High Passes Trek (19 Days), and short itineraries like Everest View Trek (7 Days).
Which trek is best for first-time Everest trekkers?
The standard 14-day Everest Base Camp Trek or the 7-day Everest View Trek are ideal for first-timers with good cardiovascular fitness.
Is Everest region trekking difficult?
Treks in the Everest region range from moderate to challenging. Altitude is the main factor, with key viewpoints like Kala Patthar reaching 5,545m.
What is the best time to enter the Khumbu?
Spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) offer clear mountain views, comfortable daytime temperatures, and stable weather.
Do I need a guide for Everest region trekking?
Yes, trekking with a licensed Sherpa guide is highly recommended for safety, altitude monitoring, logistics management, and rich local cultural insights.
What permits are required?
You require two permits: the Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality Permit (NPR 3,000) and the Sagarmatha National Park Entry Permit (NPR 3,000). Both are arranged by Igloo Himalaya Treks.
What is the highest altitude reached?
The highest trekking viewpoint is Kala Patthar at 5,545m, followed by Everest Base Camp at 5,364m and high passes like Kongma La at 5,535m.
Can I combine trekking with peak climbing?
Yes! Island Peak (6,189m), Lobuche East (6,119m), and Mera Peak (6,476m) can all be combined with Everest Base Camp treks.
