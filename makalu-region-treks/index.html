Why Trek in Makalu?

  • Mount Makalu (8,485m) — famous four-sided pyramid peak
  • Barun Valley National Park sanctuary with over 3,000 flowering plants
  • Sheer granite cliffs, hanging glaciers, and roaring waterfalls
  • True isolation away from commercial tea-house trails
  • Cross high alpine Shipton La Pass (4,220m)
  • Authentic Rai and Sherpa high mountain hospitality

Best Time to Trek

Spring (Mar–May)Excellent

Altitude & Safety

Max Altitude: 4,870m (Makalu Base Camp).

Daily pulse oximeter monitoring by Sherpa guides and planned acclimatization days.
