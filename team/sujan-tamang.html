"There is so much to enjoy in Nepal, even between the big mountain views. I want my clients to have time to notice those little things too."

Sujan Tamang is a certified Trekking Guide at Igloo Himalaya Treks, originating from the scenic Himalayan foothills of Dhading, Nepal. Over the course of seven years of continuous trekking leadership, Sujan has guided more than 80 expeditions across Nepal’s most beloved mountain trails.

Having completed his Higher Secondary Education (Plus Two) and earned his official Trekking Guide Certification from the Nepal Academy of Tourism and Hotel Management (NATHM), Sujan brings a thoughtful, educated, and deeply responsible approach to client care. He is particularly celebrated by solo hikers and trekking groups for his intuitive awareness of walkers’ energy—always checking on tiredness, dynamically adjusting the pace, and ensuring everyone feels safe, comfortable, and never rushed.

Sujan’s extensive trail experience includes 30+ treks in Annapurna, 20+ treks in Everest, 20+ treks in Langtang, and 10+ treks in Manaslu. His favourite routes are the amphitheatre of Annapurna Base Camp and the rich cultural valley of Langtang.

Fluent in English, Hindi, and Nepali, Sujan goes above and beyond to introduce travellers to local cuisine, village traditions, and authentic Himalayan hospitality, turning every trek into a warm and unforgettable cultural journey.

“

There is so much to enjoy in Nepal, even between the big mountain views. I want my clients to have time to notice those little things too. When you walk at the right pace, listen to your body, and connect with local villagers, a trek becomes something you remember for the rest of your life.

— Sujan Tamang, Trekking Guide
