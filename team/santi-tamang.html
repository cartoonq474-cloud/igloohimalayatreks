Santi Tamang is the Executive Director of Igloo Himalaya Treks Pvt. Ltd. She holds a Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) and a master’s degree in Business.

In her executive leadership role, Santi guides the company’s strategic development and oversees its daily operations, helping the team turn travel plans into well-organised journeys. Her academic background in business administration and strategic management provides strong operational leadership to the agency’s rapidly expanding portfolio of Himalayan trekking, mountaineering, and cultural tours.

Her work focuses on service quality and traveller care as the company grows its trekking and tourism services in Nepal. From coordinating between ground guides, logistics dispatchers, and international partners to maintaining uncompromising standards of guest safety and satisfaction, Santi ensures that every client experiences Nepal with peace of mind.
