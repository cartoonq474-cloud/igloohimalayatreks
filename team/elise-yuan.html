"Exploring Langtang Valley alongside Surya Lama was an unforgettable adventure that revealed the true heart of Himalayan hospitality. Today, I am proud to help travellers from the United States begin their own Himalayan journeys."

Elise Yuan first explored Langtang Valley with Surya Lama, CEO of Igloo Himalaya Treks. That journey began her connection with the company and gave her firsthand experience of trekking in Nepal.

Trekking through alpine pine forests, crossing scenic suspension bridges, and ascending into the glacial amphitheatre of Kyanjin Gompa, Elise experienced the meticulous safety protocols, heartfelt warmth, and high standard of guiding that define Igloo Himalaya Treks.

As its representative in the United States, she now introduces travellers to Himalayan adventures and connects them directly with the Igloo team to plan personalized, seamless mountain expeditions.
