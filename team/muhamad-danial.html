"Nepal offers an unparalleled mountain experience. As part of Igloo Himalaya Treks, my goal is to ensure every Malaysian adventurer steps onto Himalayan trails with confidence, safety, and authentic local support."

Muhamad Danial represents Igloo Himalaya Treks in Malaysia. He is an integral part of the company’s international representative team, providing a trusted Malaysian connection to its comprehensive trekking, peak-climbing, and tour services in Nepal.

For Malaysian hikers and mountaineers planning to journey from Southeast Asia’s tropical climate to the sub-zero heights of the Himalayas, Danial bridges the preparation gap. He offers practical guidance on high-altitude gear selection, physical conditioning, acclimatization schedules, visa requirements, and direct booking coordination with the Kathmandu head office.

Through his dedicated work, travellers from Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor, and across Malaysia enjoy transparent pricing, personalized itinerary customization, and direct access to licensed Sherpa guides and safety-certified mountain leaders.
