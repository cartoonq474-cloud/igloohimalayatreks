Mingma Sherpa grew up in the remote Rolwaling Valley (Beding). He combines deep high-altitude guiding experience with Wilderness First Responder medical qualifications.

Mingma has led treks across Dolpo, Kanchenjunga, Rolwaling, and Langtang, maintaining strict medical monitoring for off-the-beaten-path expeditions.
