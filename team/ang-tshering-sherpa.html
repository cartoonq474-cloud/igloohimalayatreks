"Born in Pangboche, carrying loads at age 18 to leading direct Sherpa operations."

Ang Tshering Sherpa grew up in the high Himalayan village of Pangboche, Solukhumbu, right under the shadow of Mt. Ama Dablam. He began his mountain career in 1999 as an expedition porter, carrying duffel bags across Everest Base Camp and Cho La Pass. Over the next decade, through discipline and technical training, Ang qualified as a certified lead guide and high-altitude expedition leader.

Between 2008 and 2018, Ang achieved 5 successful summits of Mt. Everest (8,848.86m) and led over 50 expeditions across Everest, Annapurna, and Manaslu. In 2011, he founded Igloo Himalaya Treks to eliminate middleman brokers, guarantee fair 100% porter wages, and provide foreign trekkers with honest high-altitude safety management.
