Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions before reaching out? Everything you need to know about response times, office visits, custom itineraries, and bookings.

How quickly will I receive a reply to my inquiry or route consultation?

▼

Our Kathmandu senior trek team replies to all website inquiries and emails within 2–4 hours during regular office hours (9:00 AM – 7:00 PM NPT). For immediate emergency queries or real-time planning, our WhatsApp (+977 9860843980) operates 24/7/365 with typical responses in under 15–30 minutes.

Do you charge any consultation fee for custom itinerary planning?

▼

Never. All initial consultations, route feasibility assessments, altitude acclimation profiling, and detailed quote proposals from our Senior Sherpa Guides are 100% complimentary with zero obligation to book.

Can I visit your office in Kathmandu before my trek starts?

▼

Yes, absolutely! We warmly welcome travelers to our Kathmandu headquarters in Geetanjali Chowk, Ward 16 (15 minutes north of Thamel). We host thorough pre-trek safety briefings, verify all national park permits, inspect personal gear over authentic Himalayan tea, and introduce you to your lead Sherpa guide.

Do you provide airport pickup and luggage storage in Kathmandu?

▼

Yes. Complimentary private airport pickup and drop-off at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) is included with all multi-day trek packages. We also provide secure, locked, complimentary luggage storage at our Kathmandu office or partner hotels for items you do not wish to carry onto the mountain trail.

Can you customize my itinerary, departure dates, or group size?

▼

Yes. More than 60% of our expeditions are private tailor-made journeys. Whether you need extra acclimatization days, a helicopter flight return from Everest Base Camp or Annapurna, a private solo trek, or upgraded luxury lodges, our senior operations team designs custom trips matching your exact schedule and fitness level.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions and medical preferences?

▼

Yes. We regularly cater to vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and kosher preferences during teahouse trekking and camping expeditions. Simply let us know your requirements on the direct consultation form so our team can notify lodge kitchens along the route in advance.

What payment methods do you accept for booking deposits?

▼

We accept international bank wire transfers, online credit/debit card payments (Visa, MasterCard, American Express via our secure authorized merchant gateway), and cash upon arrival in Kathmandu (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, or NPR). A 20% advance deposit confirms your guide, porter team, domestic flights, and national park permits.

What happens if my international or domestic flight gets delayed?

▼

Our Kathmandu operations team actively tracks international and domestic flight statuses in real time (including Lukla and Pokhara mountain weather windows). If your flight is delayed or postponed due to mountain weather, we immediately adjust your domestic transfers, guide schedules, and lodge reservations at zero administrative penalty.
