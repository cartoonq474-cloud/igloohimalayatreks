Lived Mountain Experience

From Solukhumbu Trails to Direct Expedition Operations

Our founder, Ang Tshering Sherpa, grew up in the high Himalayan valley of Solukhumbu. Like most young Sherpas in the Everest region, he began his career at age 18 carrying duffel bags as a porter, learning the trails, altitude dynamics, and weather patterns from old expedition veterans.

Over the next decade, Ang advanced to assistant guide, licensed lead mountain guide, and eventually led multi-summit high-altitude peak expeditions. However, he noticed a persistent problem in international mountain tourism: overseas agencies charging high commissions while local guides and porters received inadequate equipment and low wages.

Why We Founded Igloo Himalaya Treks:

To create a direct, local Sherpa-owned agency where trekkers receive honest high-altitude advice, safety is never compromised for profit, porters are treated ethically, and local Himalayan communities directly benefit.

Nepal Tourism Board (NTB) Regulatory Requirement

Under Nepal Tourism Board regulations, foreign trekkers on major routes (including Everest, Annapurna, Manaslu, Mustang, and Langtang) are required to travel with a government-licensed guide registered through a certified local agency. Working with Igloo Himalaya Treks ensures 100% legal compliance, official TIMS registration, and emergency rescue insurance.

Safety Framework

Our Approach to Mountain Safety

High altitude requires operational discipline, medical equipment, and clear protocols.

1

Before the Trek

  • Comprehensive fitness & health evaluation
  • Required high-altitude equipment audit
  • Acclimatization profile verification
  • Matching group with compatible guide
2

During the Trek

  • Twice-daily pulse oximeter SpO2 logs
  • Strict elevation gain caps (300-500m/day above 3,000m)
  • Garmin inReach satellite tracking
  • Boiled/filtered drinking water protocols
3

In an Emergency

  • Immediate descent to lower altitude
  • First-aid & portable altitude chamber / O2
  • Direct satellite dispatch to Kathmandu hub
  • Helicopter evacuation & hospital transfer

Ethical Mountain Tourism

100% Fair Porter & Staff Welfare Policy

Porters are the backbone of Himalayan trekking. We adhere strictly to the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN) and IPKO ethical guidelines:

  • 20kg Weight Limit: Strict maximum load cap per porter.
  • Living Wages: Fair daily wages above industry averages.
  • High-Altitude Gear: Warm jackets, trousers, gloves, and boots provided.
  • Medical & Rescue Insurance: Comprehensive insurance covering emergency helicopter evacuation.
  • Warm Teahouse Shelter: Guaranteed warm sleeping arrangements and meals.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns and operates Igloo Himalaya Treks?

We are 100% locally owned and operated by veteran Sherpa mountaineer Ang Tshering Sherpa (5x Everest summitter) and based in Thamel, Kathmandu, Nepal.

Are you a government-registered trekking agency?

Yes, we hold Nepal Ministry of Tourism License #1482/068, Company Registration #60124859, and active memberships with TAAN and NMA.

What qualifications do your mountain guides have?

All our lead guides hold government trekking licenses from NMA/TAAN, high-altitude wilderness first-responder training, and a minimum of 8–15 years experience in the Himalayas.

How do you handle altitude sickness (AMS)?

Our guides monitor guest pulse oximeter SpO2 logs twice daily, carry emergency oxygen, follow strict elevation gain limits, and coordinate immediate descent or helicopter evacuation if symptoms persist.

How does trip deposit and cancellation work?

We require a 20% advance deposit to secure permits and domestic flights. Deposits can be transferred to future travel dates if weather or personal plans change.
