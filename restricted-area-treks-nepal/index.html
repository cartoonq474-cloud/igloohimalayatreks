Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Restricted Area Trek in Nepal?
Restricted areas are regulated border zones requiring special government permits (RAP) and an official licensed guide.
Can a solo traveler trek in restricted areas?
No. Government regulation requires a minimum of 2 trekkers booked through a registered Nepal agency.
