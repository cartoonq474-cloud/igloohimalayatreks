Payment Currency Advice: Always Carry Cash USD

While Rastriya Banijya Bank card terminals officially accept major credit cards (Visa & Mastercard) and major currencies (EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD), electronic network timeouts and card reader glitches happen regularly at Kathmandu Airport. Carrying exact, crisp, unmarked US Dollar banknotes ($10, $20, $50, or $100 bills) guarantees you will clear the bank queue in under 3 minutes without card surcharge fees.

Step-by-Step Arrival Procedure at Kathmandu Airport (KTM)

Once your flight touches down at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM), enter the international arrival hall and proceed through these 3 sequential stations:

1

Digital Pre-Registration & Electronic Kiosks Entry Hall

Option A (Fastest & Strongly Recommended): Pre-fill your online visa registration up to 14 days before flying at the official Nepal Immigration portal: nepaliport.immigration.gov.np. Save or print the confirmation barcode slip.
Option B: If you did not register online before boarding, step over to the row of touchscreen kiosks located directly in front of the arrival aerobridge. Scan your passport photo page and let the camera take your photo to generate your printed barcode receipt.

💡 Pro-Tip from Igloo Guides: Filling the form on your smartphone while waiting at your transit airport (e.g., Doha, Dubai, or Singapore) lets you walk completely past the crowded kiosk line straight to Station 2!
2

Pay Visa Fee at Rastriya Banijya Bank Counter Bank Desk

Walk to the Rastriya Banijya Bank payment counters located directly to the left of the immigration gates. Hand over your barcode slip and exact visa fee ($30 USD for 15 days, $50 USD for 30 days, or $125 USD for 90 days). The teller will stamp your barcode paper and issue a physical, serialized bank payment slip.

💡 Pro-Tip from Igloo Guides: Inspect your USD bills before traveling. Make sure they have no pen marks, tears, or stamps. Series 2013 and newer bills are accepted effortlessly.
3

Immigration Officer Inspection Desk & Entry Stamp Final Gate

Join the designated queues marked "Tourist Visa on Arrival" (or "Online Form Submissions" if pre-filled). Hand the Immigration Officer: (1) Your Passport, (2) The Bank Payment Receipt, and (3) The Barcode Registration Slip. The officer verifies your details, sticks the full-page visa sticker into your passport, applies the entry date stamp, and hands your passport back.

🎉 Welcome to Nepal! Walk down the escalator, collect your checked trekking duffel bag from the baggage carousel, and walk out to the exit gates where your Igloo Himalaya Treks airport representative will be holding your personalized name board!

Mandatory Entry Documents Checklist

Keep the following 6 essentials inside your carry-on daypack so you don't need to dig through checked baggage inside the arrival hall:

6-Month Valid Passport

Must have at least 6 months remaining validity from your arrival date and at least two blank consecutive visa stamp pages.

Printed Barcode Slip

Generated after completing your application on nepaliport.immigration.gov.np. A clear screenshot on your phone also works.

Visa Fee in Cash (USD)

Exact change in crisp, unmarked US Dollars ($30 for 15 days, $50 for 30 days, or $125 for 90 days). EUR and GBP are also accepted at the counter rate.

Hotel Address in Kathmandu

Name, ward, and district of your hotel in Kathmandu (e.g., Thamel, Ward 16, Kathmandu). Igloo Himalaya Treks provides your hotel confirmation voucher.

Return Flight Ticket Itinerary

Immigration officers occasionally ask for digital proof of onward or return departure from Nepal within your allowed visa validity window.

Physical Passport Photos (Land Borders)

Not needed at Kathmandu Airport (kiosks scan digitally), but carry 2 physical passport-sized photos if crossing into Nepal overland from India.

Nationalities & Visa Eligibility Matrix

Nepal maintains one of the world's most accessible on-arrival visa frameworks. Check which category applies to your passport:

✓ Eligible on Arrival

Western & Global Travelers

Citizens from over 100 countries receive tourist visas on arrival at Kathmandu Airport (KTM) or designated land checkposts with zero advance embassy application required.

Includes: USA, Canada, United Kingdom, European Union / EEA countries, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, Switzerland, Norway, UAE, and most worldwide passports.
🛂 Visa-Exempt Entry

Indian Passport Holders

Under the 1950 Indo-Nepal Treaty of Peace and Friendship, Indian citizens do not require a visa to enter, travel, or trek anywhere in Nepal.

Mandatory ID: When flying into KTM, you must present either an original valid Indian Passport or an official Election Commission Voter Identity Card with photo. Aadhaar and PAN cards are not accepted for flight immigration.
⭐ Gratis (Free) Visa

SAARC Member Nationals & Children

Citizens of SAARC member nations receive a 30-day tourist visa free of charge (gratis) for their first visit within a single calendar year (Jan 1 to Dec 31).

Eligible SAARC Nations: Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka. Children under 10 years of all nationalities are also granted free gratis visas.
⚠️ Embassy Visa Required

Non-Eligible for On-Arrival

Passport holders from 11 countries cannot obtain visas on arrival and must secure their visa through an official Nepalese diplomatic mission/embassy prior to departure.

Restricted Passports: Nigeria, Ghana, Zimbabwe, Swaziland, Cameroon, Somalia, Liberia, Ethiopia, Iraq, Palestine, Syria, and Afghanistan.

Entering Nepal Overland: Land Border Checkposts

If you are traveling overland into Nepal from India or Tibet (China), on-arrival tourist visas are officially issued at these designated immigration border checkposts:

Belahiya (Sunauli)
India Border • Rupandehi District

Most popular overland route from Gorakhpur (Uttar Pradesh) to Lumbini, Pokhara, and Kathmandu. Open daily from 06:00 to 22:00.

Kakarbhitta
India Border • Jhapa District

Eastern entry point connected to Siliguri, Darjeeling, and Sikkim (West Bengal). Excellent for overland travel to Kanchenjunga region.

Birgunj (Raxaul)
India Border • Parsa District

Central commercial border point connected to Bihar. Direct highway buses and shared jeeps connect to Kathmandu in 5–6 hours.

Jamunaha (Nepalgunj)
India Border • Banke District

Western gateway from Lucknow (Uttar Pradesh). The primary launching hub for domestic flights to Dolpo, Rara Lake, and Simikot (Humla).

Mohana (Dhangadhi)
India Border • Kailali District

Far-western Nepal gateway connecting Uttar Pradesh to Suklaphanta National Park, Khaptad, and pristine western wilderness trails.

Rasuwagadhi (Kerung)
China / Tibet Border • Rasuwa District

Northern border connecting Lhasa (Tibet Autonomous Region) to Langtang Valley and Kathmandu. Note: Requires approved Tibet group travel permits.

Essential Advice for Overland Borders

Electronic card payment terminals and touchscreen kiosks are NOT available at land border immigration desks. You MUST carry exact cash in US Dollars (or Indian Rupees where permitted) and two physical passport-sized photos with white backgrounds.

Where to Apply for an Extension:

  • Department of Immigration (Kathmandu): Located in Kalikasthan, Dillibazar, Kathmandu. Open Sunday to Friday (10:00 to 16:00). Processing takes 1–2 hours.
  • Immigration Office (Pokhara): Located in Sahid Chowk, Pokhara. Perfect if you finish the Annapurna Circuit or Mardi Himal and want to add another trek.
  • Avoid Overstaying: Overstaying your visa without an official extension carries a penalty fee of $5 USD per day plus the regular extension fee, payable at the immigration office before departure.
