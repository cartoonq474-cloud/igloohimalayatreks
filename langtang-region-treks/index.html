Why Trek in Langtang?

  • Accessible starting point just a 6-hour drive from Kathmandu
  • Breathtaking views of Langtang Lirung (7,234m) and Dorje Lakpa
  • Unique Tamang Buddhist heritage and authentic village homestays
  • Sacred high-altitude Gosaikunda alpine lakes
  • Traditional yak cheese factory at Kanjin Gompa
  • Quiet, scenic alternative to crowded EBC and Annapurna trails

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Langtang from Kathmandu?
Langtang trailhead at Syabrubesi is a 6-8 hour drive by private jeep or bus from Kathmandu.
Is Langtang Valley trek difficult?
Langtang Valley is a moderate trek, ideal for beginners with good basic fitness.
What is the highest altitude on Langtang Valley trek?
Kanjin Gompa is at 3,870m, and optional day hikes to Tserko Ri reach 4,984m.
What permits do I need for Langtang?
You need the Langtang National Park Entry Permit and TIMS Card.
