Traveler Safety & Protection

Why Legal Registration Protects Your Trek

Trekking with an unregistered operator or informal freelancer carries severe legal, financial, and medical risks. Here is how our certified accreditation safeguards your journey:

Guaranteed Emergency Helicopter Evacuation

In the event of severe Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) or trauma above 4,000 meters, helicopter charter companies and the Nepal Army require an authorized agency guarantee before launching. Unregistered freelancers cannot dispatch life-saving flights.

Valid National Park & Restricted Permits

Under Nepal Tourism Board (NTB) regulations, solo trekking without a registered agency is prohibited in most regions. Our license authorizes instant issuance of TIMS cards, Sagarmatha National Park entry, and Department of Immigration restricted permits (Manaslu, Mustang, Dolpo).

Mandatory Crew Life & Medical Insurance

Nepali labor law strictly mandates that all licensed agencies provide comprehensive high-altitude medical and emergency rescue insurance for their Sherpa guides and porters. We guarantee 100% fair wages and ethical treatment according to IPPG standards.

Central Bank Protected Escrow Payments

Under Nepal Rastra Bank guidelines, international client deposits are received into supervised corporate accounts at Himalayan Bank Ltd., ensuring your funds are legally bonded, secure against fraud, and fully receipted with government tax invoices.
